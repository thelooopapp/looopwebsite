What carrier apps do well

They're built to help parents manage a child's phone.

Verizon Family

Location, alerts, check-ins, Safe Walk SOS, crash detection alerts, roadside help, content filters, screen time, driving insights, and support for Gizmo watches. Its release notes mention Adult Check-Ins.

AT&T Secure Family

A family map with location history, arrival alerts, check-ins, a driving score, crash alerts, a family emergency alert, and content filters, for up to 10 members and 30 devices.

T-Mobile FamilyMode

Screen time, content filters, check-in, location, and SOS, built to help parents look after a child's phone.

Clean privacy labels

None of the three declares "Data Used to Track You" on its App Store privacy label.

"We already pay our carrier. Why add Looop?"

What Looop does for grown-up families

Mom's phone plan doesn't matter.
Looop lets you know when it's a good day to call her.

  • Mom's whole day, in one answer

    Looop takes a holistic approach to family care: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day. Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

    Severe weather alerts reach more than 50 countries, and nearby crime alerts cover all 50 US states, at 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles. Health & Fitness sharing is available through Apple Health (Apple Watch, Oura, or Whoop), and so is an optional heads-up about an unusually large charge on a linked account.

  • Nothing to check until it matters

    When all is well, you won't hear from Looop. When it might be a good time to check in, you get a nudge. You can open the family map whenever you need it.

  • Built for grown-up families, on any carrier

    Maybe it's a parent a few states away, kids at college or in a big city, or siblings and friends who look out for each other. Every Loved One joins by invitation and keeps their routine.

    Looop works on any carrier, so a parent on one phone plan and a sibling on another can be in the same Looop.

  • Clean labels, and no ads

    Like the three carrier apps, Looop's App Store privacy label has no "Data Used to Track You" section. Looop also never sells your data, doesn't work with advertisers, and has no ads.

    Spending alerts run through Plaid if you turn them on, and that data is never used for ads or sold.

Where carrier apps may fit better

For a child's phone, a carrier app may fit better.

A young child's phone

If you want to set screen time and filter content on a child's phone, these apps are built for that. Looop doesn't do parental controls.

Crash alerts and roadside help

Verizon Family lists crash detection alerts and roadside help, and AT&T Secure Family lists crash alerts. Looop doesn't detect crashes.

Already in your plan

If your Verizon postpaid plan includes basic location, there's nothing extra to pay for it.

A kid's smartwatch

Verizon Family supports Gizmo watches.

Questions about carrier apps and Looop

Does Looop work on any carrier?

Yes. Looop works on iPhone with any carrier, so everyone in your Looop can be on a different phone plan.

Is Looop a parental control app?

No. Looop doesn't set screen time or filter content. It's built for grown-up families and tells you when it might be a good time to check in.

Can I use Looop with Verizon Family, AT&T Secure Family, or T-Mobile FamilyMode?

Yes. A carrier app can help with a young child's phone, and Looop can let you know when it might be time to call Mom.

Does Looop work for aging parents?

Yes. Looop was made for adult children who live far from a parent, and for siblings and friends who look out for each other. Your parent can be on any carrier. They join by invitation and keep their routine, and you get a heads-up when it might be a good time to call.

How much does Looop cost?

Looop is free for the first two weeks. It's available for iPhone on the App Store.
