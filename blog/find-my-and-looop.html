What each one does

Apple Find My shows where people, devices, and AirTags are, and it's free on every iPhone.
Looop is a safety notifications app that works alongside it. It sends one daily answer about Mom's day, with nothing for her to start.

What Apple's tools do well

Apple's tools are free and come built into every iPhone.

Free and already there

Find My comes on every iPhone. If Mom has one, there's nothing to buy or download.

Phones, keys, and AirPods

Find My finds lost Apple devices and AirTags, and you can share an AirTag with up to five people.

Help with no signal

On iPhone 14 and later, Find My can share location over satellite when there's no cell service or Wi-Fi.

Check In for one trip

According to Apple's guide, Check In in Messages lets someone tell a friend or family member they got home, for a walk home or a drive across town.

Health Sharing

Apple says a person can share Health data with family in the Health app.

Privacy you can check

Find My's App Store label has no "Data Used to Track You" section.

"So what does Looop add?"

What Looop adds to Apple's tools

Five things, all aimed at knowing when to call.

  • All in one place

    Apple's tools live in separate places.
    Looop puts weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of her day in one place.

    You don't have to open Find My, then Messages, then the Health app. Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

  • Looop speaks up on its own

    You don't have to open Looop to hear from it.
    It tells you about a storm heading her way, crime reported nearby, or a day that looks unusual.

  • Built for parents and the people who love them

    Looop is built for adult children caring from a distance, and for siblings who want the same heads-up. See everyone you care about on one screen, and know at a glance who's okay.

  • Nothing for Mom to start

    Apple says Check In works when the person starts a session. Looop works quietly in the background once she's joined, so there are no sessions, timers, or check-ins for her to remember.

  • No advertisers, and nothing for sale

    Like Find My, Looop's App Store privacy label has no "Data Used to Track You" section. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers.

Looop isn't an emergency service. It doesn't call 911 for you, send help, or detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

"How would we use both?"

How families use both

Mom keeps the Apple tools she already uses.
Looop adds a heads-up about her day.

  • Keep Find My for things and trips

    Mom keeps sharing her location in Find My, finding her phone, and using Check In for a late drive home, just as she does now.

  • Add Looop for the day

    Looop sends you one heads-up when a storm heads toward her, when crime is reported near her, or when her day looks unusual. Quiet means all is well.

  • Then reach out

    When Looop nudges you, call her. If you want to see where she is, open Find My or Looop's family map.

"Do we need Looop at all?"

Where Find My may be enough

Sometimes the free option already covers it.

You just want to see where she is

If a location on a map answers your question, Find My does that for free.

Lost phones and keys

Find My finds devices and AirTags. Looop doesn't help you find things.

Places with no signal

One trip at a time

If you mostly worry about one drive or one walk home, Check In may be all you need.

Questions families ask

Do I need Looop if Mom already shares her location in Find My?

Find My shows where she is when you look. Looop tells you when a storm heads her way, when crime is reported nearby, or when her day looks unusual, so you know when to call. Many families use both.

Can I use Find My and Looop together?

Yes. Looop works alongside Find My. Keep Find My for her phone, her AirTags, and sharing location. Looop runs on its own and adds the heads-up about her day.

Does Apple's Check In work for checking on a parent?

Check In works well for one walk or one drive. According to Apple's guide, the person being checked on starts each session in Messages, with a timer or a destination. Looop doesn't need Mom to start anything. Read the full answer.

Does Looop work with Apple Watch and Apple Health?

Yes. If your Loved One wears an Apple Watch, Oura Ring, or Whoop, Health & Fitness sharing through Apple Health is available if you need it. Nothing new to install on the watch.

Is Looop an emergency service?

Looop isn't an emergency service. It doesn't call 911 for you, send help, or detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.
