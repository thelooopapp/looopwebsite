What iSharing is built around

iSharing is a location sharing app built around a live map, with place alerts, location history, driving reports, and an inactivity alert it pitches for older parents.
Looop is a safety notifications app for families caring from a distance, built around one daily answer about each Loved One's day.

What iSharing does well

Its free plan covers the basics, and paid plans add driving reports.

A free plan

You can share location, set place alerts, and send messages without paying. Premium plans add more history and driving reports.

Place alerts and history

Families get alerts when someone arrives at or leaves home, work, or school, with location history from 7 days to 1 year, depending on plan.

A panic alert

A panic or SOS alert sends family an emergency message with recorded sound.

Driving reports

Gold and higher plans report speeding, sudden braking, and phone use while driving.

An idea for parents far away

Its inactivity alert speaks to a real worry for adult children: a parent who hasn't picked up the phone all morning.

Years of experience

iSharing says it has been around for more than 15 years.

"How is Looop different from iSharing?"

What Looop looks at

A daily answer, made for checking on a parent from afar.

  • Her whole day, in one place

    Looop takes a holistic approach to family care: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day.

    If her phone sits still all morning, Looop weighs that against the rest of her day before it nudges you. Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

  • Quiet until it matters

    Looop stays quiet when all is well and speaks up when Mom's day looks unusual. The family map is there for after a nudge, when you need it.

  • Made for grown families

    Looop is built for adult children caring from a distance, and for siblings and friends who look out for each other. Every Loved One joins by invitation and keeps their routine. Spending alerts and Health & Fitness sharing are available if your family needs them.

  • What the privacy policies say

    Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. Looop's App Store privacy label has no "Data Used to Track You" section.

    iSharing's privacy policy, last revised May 21, 2026, says: "We may share your personal information, including your location data, with third parties for marketing purposes." Its App Store label lists location, identifiers, and five other kinds of data as "Data Used to Track You."

Looop isn't an emergency service. It doesn't call 911 for you, send help, or detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

"Should we stick with iSharing?"

Where iSharing may fit better

If one of these sounds like you, iSharing may be the better pick.

A live map of the kids

If you want to see where young kids are through the day, with place alerts and history, iSharing is built for that.

Detailed driving reports

For a new driver, iSharing's reports on speeding, braking, and phone use go further than Looop's nudge when a drive looks unusual.

A free plan

If you want location sharing without a subscription, iSharing's free plan covers the basics. Looop is free for the first two weeks.

Finding a lost phone

iSharing includes a Find My Device feature. Looop doesn't help you find a phone.

Questions families ask

Is Looop like iSharing?

They do different jobs. iSharing is built around a live map, with place alerts, location history, and driving reports. Looop is a safety notifications app. It brings weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day together, and stays quiet when all is well.

Can I use iSharing and Looop together?

Yes. Some families keep a map app for the kids and use Looop for a parent who lives far away. Looop works on its own, and nothing in it depends on iSharing.

Does iSharing work for elderly parents?

iSharing offers what it calls "Elderly Care": an alert when an older person doesn't use their phone. Looop is built for checking on a parent from a distance, and a quiet phone is just one part of the day it looks at. Read the full answer.

Does Looop sell my data?

No. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. Bank linking is optional, runs through Plaid, and is never used for ads.

Is Looop an emergency service?

Looop isn't an emergency service. It doesn't call 911 for you, send help, or detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.
