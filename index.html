"Would they tell you if something was off?"

Out of the ordinary? You'll know.

Safety alerts about Unusual Days. A nudge when something seems amiss. On a typical day, Looop stays quiet.

Home longer than usual?

If Mom has been home much longer than is normal for her, you get a gentle nudge.

At the hospital?

If Dad arrives at a hospital or ER, you get a heads-up, so you can call and ask how he's doing.

Phone quiet too long?

If Aunt Ira's phone sits untouched far longer than usual, one of you can check in.

Looop doesn't detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911.
