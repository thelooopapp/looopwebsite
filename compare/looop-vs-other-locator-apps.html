Each app, fairly

What each one does well, and who it may suit better than Looop.

Famio

What it does well. Famio, from Gismart Ltd, puts a lot in one app: location history, places, battery alerts, SOS, a Driving Mode, a Child Mode, and breach alerts. Its listing says both sides must agree to share.

Where it may fit better. Families with kids or new drivers who want those tools together, on a weekly plan or a lifetime purchase. Famio on the App Store

Familo

What it does well. Familo, from SAFETIX LTD, keeps it simple: a family map, places, a panic button, check-ins, and chat. Its listing says all members consent to sharing.

Where it may fit better. Families who want a basic shared map with a panic button, on a weekly or yearly plan. Familo on the App Store

Carpin

What it does well. Carpin, from Monoyoga Yazilim A.S., shows live locations, sends place alerts when someone arrives or leaves, keeps a location history, and lets members send SOS messages and check-ins with a note or photo.

Where it may fit better. Family and friends who want a shared map with check-ins and battery levels. Carpin on the App Store

Be Closer

What it does well. Be Closer, from SUPERSSTAR LABS, S.L., is made for parents of kids and teens, including young drivers, with places, location history, and SOS. Its listing calls it "a mutual consent-based service."

Where it may fit better. Parents of kids and new drivers who want place alerts and an SOS button. Be Closer on the App Store

Find Phone: Device Tracker

What it does well. Find Phone, from Webpeak OU, offers real-time location for a private circle, location history, place alerts, SOS alerts, and help finding a lost phone. Its listing says it "cannot be installed remotely or without consent."

Where it may fit better. People who mainly want help finding a lost phone and a small circle on a map. Find Phone on the App Store

Find my Phone – Family Locator

What it does well. Find my Phone, from GEO TRACK LABS LTD, has been around since about 2012. It offers place alerts, seven days of location history on the free version, check-ins, and SOS calls on premium. Its listing says sharing is "only possible upon mutual consent of all members."

Where it may fit better. Families who want a long-running locator they can try for free. Find my Phone on the App Store

Blink

What it does well. Blink, from Blinkmap Limited, is a social map for friends, with chat, video calls, and step counts.

Where it may fit better. Teens and friends who want to see each other on a map and hang out in the app. It's made for friends more than for family safety. Blink on the App Store

What these apps do well

They share a few real strengths.

Easy to start

Most offer a free download, a short trial, or a one-time lifetime option.

SOS and help alerts

Famio, Familo, Carpin, Be Closer, Find Phone, and Find my Phone all list an SOS, panic, or help alert.

Places and history

Arrival and departure alerts at places you save, and a record of where someone has been.

Consent, stated up front

Most of these listings say both people must agree before anything is shared, several in plain words.

"Seven map apps. What does Looop do?"

What Looop does

These apps answer "Where is she?"
Looop answers "Is Mom okay today?"

  • A whole day, in one answer

    Looop takes a holistic approach to family care: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day. Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

    Severe weather alerts work in more than 50 countries, and nearby crime alerts work in all 50 US states, at 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles. Health & Fitness sharing through Apple Health (Apple Watch, Oura, or Whoop) is available if you need it, and so is an optional heads-up about an unusually large charge on a linked account.

  • A heads-up when something looks off

    Looop doesn't ping you all day. It sends a nudge when it might be a good time to check in. There's a family map for when you need it.

  • Made for families spread across the country

    Looop is made for families who live far apart, with a parent a few states away, kids at college or in a big city, and siblings and friends who look out for each other. Everyone joins by invitation and keeps their routine.

  • What each label declares

    Looop never sells your data, doesn't work with advertisers, and has no ads. Its App Store privacy label has no "Data Used to Track You" section and doesn't say the app contains advertising.

    Each of the seven other apps declares "Data Used to Track You" on its App Store label, in Apple's words, and most sell weekly plans. Looop is free for the first two weeks.

    Optional spending alerts go through Plaid, and that data is never used for ads or sold.

"What if one of these fits us better?"

Where these apps may fit better

Here's when one of them may be the better pick.

Kids and new drivers

Famio's Child Mode and Driving Mode, and Be Closer's focus on young drivers, are built for that stage. Looop is made for grown-up families.

An SOS button

Six of these apps list an SOS, panic, or help alert. Looop has no SOS button and isn't an emergency service.

Finding a lost phone

Find Phone and Find my Phone help locate a lost phone. Looop doesn't.

Friends hanging out

Blink is a social map with chat and video calls. Looop is quieter on purpose.

Questions about these locator apps

Are these apps like Looop?

They're built differently. These apps put family or friends on a live map, usually with place alerts, history, and an SOS alert. Looop is a safety notifications app that tells you when it might be a good time to check in.

Which one is best for checking on a parent?

Most of these apps are built for kids, teens, couples, or friends. Looop is built for adult children and the parents they love. For more options, read the best apps to check on an aging parent.

What does "Data Used to Track You" mean?

It's a section of an app's App Store privacy label. The developer lists data that may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. Read what "Data Used to Track You" means.

Does Looop work for aging parents?

Yes. Looop is made for checking on a parent from a distance, and for siblings and friends who look out for each other. Your parent joins by invitation and keeps their routine, and you get a heads-up when it might be a good time to call.

How much does Looop cost?

Looop is free for the first two weeks. It's available for iPhone on the App Store.
