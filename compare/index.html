Where Looop fits

Most family apps are built around a map: they answer "Where is she?" Looop answers "Is Mom okay today?"

Looop is a new kind of family safety app, made by an independent Austin startup that launched in May 2026. It takes a holistic approach to family care: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day. Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

Questions families ask

What's the difference between a family locator and a safety notifications app?

A family locator is built around a map: it answers "Where is she?" with live location, place alerts, and history. A safety notifications app like Looop answers "Is Mom okay today?" It sends a heads-up about weather, crime, or a day that looks unusual, and stays quiet when all is well.

Which family safety app is best for checking on an aging parent?

It depends on what you need. Apple Find My is free and already on her iPhone if you just want to see where she is. OnStar Guardian connects her to emergency help on the road. Looop is built for knowing when to call: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of her day, in one daily answer. See our picks.

What does "Data Used to Track You" mean on an App Store label?

It's the section of an app's App Store privacy label where the developer declares data that may be linked with data from other companies, for example for targeted ads. Most of the family locator apps in this table declare it. Looop's label has no "Data Used to Track You" section. Read more.

Is Looop an emergency service?

No. Looop doesn't call 911 for you, send help, or detect falls or crashes. Apps like Life360's paid plans and OnStar Guardian offer emergency dispatch or crash response. Looop helps you know when to check in. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.
