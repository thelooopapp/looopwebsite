Does iSharing Work for Elderly Parents?

By the Looop Team·October 26, 2026·3 min read

It can. iSharing is a location sharing app, and it does pitch adult children who live far from a parent. Its "Elderly Care" feature sends an alert when an older person doesn't use their phone, and it adds SOS, place alerts, and driving reports.

Before you invite Mom, it helps to know what each plan includes and what iSharing's privacy policy says.

What iSharing offers for aging parents

From iSharing's website and App Store listing, checked October 26, 2026:

  • Inactivity alerts: iSharing calls this "Elderly Care." Its November 2025 release, "Protecting Elderly Parents This Winter," leads with this feature for adult children who live far from their parents.
  • SOS: a panic alert with recorded sound.
  • Place alerts: for places like home, work, and school.
  • Location history: from 7 days to a year, depending on the plan.
  • Battery and inactivity alerts on Silver and up, and driving reports (speeding, sudden braking, phone use) on Gold and up.

There's a free plan. On the App Store, paid plans include $7.99 or $14.99 a month and $69.99 or $99.99 a year, with a seven-day free trial.

What its privacy policy says

iSharing's privacy policy, last revised May 21, 2026, says: "We may also share location information with third parties for advertising, research, and analytic purposes." Its App Store privacy label declares "Data Used to Track You" for purchases, location, contact info, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, and other data.

What it does well

If Mom's phone has gone quiet, that's worth a call, and an inactivity alert will tell you. iSharing also has SOS, driving reports, and a free tier, which some families want.
