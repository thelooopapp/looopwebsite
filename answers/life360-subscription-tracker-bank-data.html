What Does Life360's Subscription Tracker Do With Bank Data?

By the Looop Team·October 26, 2026·3 min read

Life360's Subscription Tracker connects your checking accounts and credit cards through Plaid and finds the recurring charges on them. Life360's own setup page also says that by connecting, you agree that "Life360 may use your transaction data to show you relevant ads and measure ad performance."

Here's what the feature does, and what to ask before you link an account to any family app.

What Subscription Tracker does

Life360 introduced Subscription Tracker on its blog on May 28, 2026. It says the feature "pulls in every recurring charge across your Circle automatically," and sends alerts for price changes, new subscriptions, and renewals. It's "included in Life360 at no extra cost."

According to Life360's help page:

  • It's for US members 18 and older.
  • It connects checking accounts and credit cards through Plaid.
  • You choose which Circle members can see your subscriptions.

Neither page mentions alerts for unusually large charges, fraud, or scams.

What Life360's terms say about your transaction data

Life360's Set Up Subscription Tracker page says that by connecting an account, you agree that "Life360 may use your transaction data to show you relevant ads and measure ad performance."

That's worth reading before anyone in the family links a card. Our guide before you link Mom's bank account covers the other questions to ask.

What it does well

Subscription Tracker puts every recurring charge in one list, flags price hikes and renewals, and costs nothing extra. For a household juggling streaming services and memberships, that's genuinely useful.

How Looop handles spending

Looop is a safety notifications app. Its spending alerts are optional: if a Loved One chooses to link an account, Looop can send the family a heads-up about an unusually large charge, which can be an early sign of a scam. The family sets the threshold.

Bank linking in Looop runs through Plaid, and that data is never used for ads or sold. Looop doesn't work with advertisers.

Spending is one part of Looop's holistic approach to family care: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day, all in one place.

Questions families ask

Is Life360's Subscription Tracker free?

Yes. Life360's blog says it's "included in Life360 at no extra cost." Its help page says it's for US members 18 and older.

What accounts does Subscription Tracker connect?

Checking accounts and credit cards, through Plaid, according to Life360's help page.

Does Looop use bank data for ads?

No. Bank linking in Looop is optional and runs through Plaid. It's used only for the family's spending alerts, and it's never used for ads or sold.

A heads-up, never an ad slot.

Optional spending alerts, never used for ads. Free for the first two weeks.
