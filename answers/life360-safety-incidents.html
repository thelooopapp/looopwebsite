What Do Life360's Safety Incidents Show?

By the Looop Team·October 26, 2026·3 min read

Life360's Safety Incidents put recent, serious events near you or someone in your Circle on the Life360 map. According to Life360's help page, they cover incidents from the last six hours, within one mile, in the US, and Life360 sends a notification when an incident meets those criteria.

So yes, Life360 has crime features. Here's what this one shows, and where its limits are.

What counts as a Safety Incident

Life360 describes them as "recent, high-severity events." Its examples include fires, thefts, and weapon-related crimes. In the app's Map details, the layer is labeled "New" and described as "Crimes, fires, police searches, and more." Each incident shows up on the map as a yellow pin.

The limits Life360 lists

  • Time: only incidents from the last six hours.
  • Distance: within one mile of you or a Circle member.
  • Place: US only.
  • Free Circles: details for up to three incidents in 24 hours. Seeing more takes a Gold or Platinum plan.
  • Data source: the help page doesn't say where the incident data comes from.

Life360's older Crime Reports feature, on paid plans, still has its own help page.

What it does well

Safety Incidents give families a quick heads-up about something serious close by, right on the map they already use. Free Circles get it too, and the six-hour window keeps it focused on what's happening now.

How Looop handles crime near a loved one

Looop is a safety notifications app. It sends a heads-up when crime is reported near a Loved One, in all 50 US states, and your family picks the distance: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles. There's no daily limit on how many alerts you can see.

Crime is one part of Looop's holistic approach to family care: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day. Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

Looop isn't an emergency service. If someone is in danger, call 911.

Questions families ask

Does Life360 have crime alerts?

Yes. Life360's Safety Incidents show recent, high-severity events within one mile of you or a Circle member on its map, with a notification when an incident meets its criteria. Its older Crime Reports feature is available on paid plans.

How close does a Safety Incident have to be?

Within one mile of you or a Circle member, from the last six hours, in the US, according to Life360's help page.

Can I choose the distance for crime alerts in Looop?

Yes. Looop sends a heads-up when crime is reported near a Loved One, in all 50 US states, at the distance your family chooses: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.
