Your family's data
isn't for sale

We never sell your data. We don't work with advertisers.

Looop is a private family safety app. Its job is to tell you when to check in, never to collect your life and sell it.

Our privacy promise, in plain words.

Seven promises, in plain language

  1. We never sell your data.

    We don't sell it to data brokers or anyone else. Some family apps have. We never will.

  2. We don't work with advertisers.

    You won't see ads in your notifications, and we have no ad partners. Families aren't the product.

  3. Health & Fitness sharing is optional.

    It's available if your family needs it, for health details like heart rate and sleep and fitness details like steps and workouts. It can be turned off anytime.

  4. Bank linking is optional, and secured through Plaid.

    Spending alerts only work if someone chooses to link an account. Skip it and everything else still works.

  5. You choose your alerts.

    Pick exactly which alerts you get about each person. Turn alerts off for anyone with one tap.

  6. You can leave anytime.

    Leave a group, remove a member, or start a new group whenever you like. Delete your account and data whenever you choose.

  7. You can talk to a real person.

    Email the people who build Looop with a question, a worry, or a new idea.

"So you'd see what I'm doing all day?"

Just enough to know

No. Looop isn't built for that.

Looop uses only what it needs to tell whether a day looks typical or unusual. It stays quiet when all is well, and speaks up when something seems off. Nothing about your family's movements is ever for sale. Your Loved Ones keep their independence. You keep your peace of mind.

"Can someone add me without asking?"

Everyone joins by invitation

No. You decide whether to join.

Nobody is added without an invite. We encourage families to talk first and walk through what's shared, side by side.
