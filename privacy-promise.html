Our Privacy Promise

We never sell your data. We don't work with advertisers. That's it.

Looop is a private family safety app. It exists to tell families when to check in. It does not exist to collect your life and sell it. Here's our promise in plain words.

Seven promises, in plain language

  1. We never sell your data.

    Not to data brokers. Not to anyone. Your family's information isn't for sale.

  2. We don't work with advertisers.

    No ads in your notifications. No ad partners. Families aren't the product.

  3. Wellness sharing is optional.

    Steps, sleep, heart rate and other health details are shared only if the Loved One chooses. They can change their mind anytime.

  4. Bank linking is optional, and secured through Plaid.

    Spending alerts only work if someone chooses to link an account. Skip it and everything else still works.

  5. You choose your alerts.

    Pick exactly which alerts you get about each person. Turn alerts off for anyone with one tap.

  6. You can leave anytime.

    Leave a group, remove a member or start a new group whenever you like. Delete your account and data whenever you choose.

  7. You can talk to a real person.

    Email the founder directly with a question, a worry or a new idea.

"So you'd see what I'm doing all day?"

Just enough to know

No. That's not the point.

Looop uses only what it needs to tell whether a day looks typical or unusual. It stays quiet when all is well, and speaks up when something seems off. Your Loved Ones keep their independence. You keep your peace of mind.

Invitations, never surprises

Every Loved One joins Looop by invitation. We encourage families to talk first and walk through what's shared, side by side. A tool someone agreed to is one they'll keep.
