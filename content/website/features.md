## How Looop works

> "Know what changed, and when to call."

1. **Invite your family.** Each family member installs Looop on their iPhone, joins your family group, and picks what to share. There's no new device to wear or charge, and nothing to fill in.
2. **Looop learns their usual routine.** Looop runs in the background on their phone. Over time, it learns what a normal day looks like for them: how much they walk and drive, and how much they use their phone. If they choose, it can also use their Apple Watch, smart ring, or bank account.
3. **You get an alert when something changes.** Looop lets you know when a storm is heading toward them, when crime is reported near them, or when their day is different from usual. The alert says what changed, so you can decide whether to call. When everything looks normal, you won't hear from Looop.

### Where Looop gets its information

- **From public sources.** Severe weather alerts from official weather agencies, and crime reported near them.
- **From their iPhone.** When they walk or drive, how much they use their phone, their battery level, and when they arrive at places you've saved, like home.
- **If they choose to share.** A checking or credit card account they connect, for unusually large purchases, and their Apple Watch or smart ring, through the Apple Health app, for heart rate, sleep, steps, and workouts.

**Note:** You see all of it in one app, instead of checking five.

## Alerts that tell you when to check in

> "Is everyone okay today?"

**Subtitle:** Looop watches for changes near them and in their routine, and lets you know when it might be time to call.

### What's happening around them

- **Severe weather.** Storms, flood warnings, extreme heat or cold heading their way, in more than 50 countries, including the US.
- **Nearby crime.** A heads-up when crime is reported near a Loved One, in all 50 US states. You pick the distance: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.

### When their day looks different

Looop compares each day to that person's own usual routine.

- **Unusual Day.** A gentle nudge when their day is different from their usual routine.
- **Unusual activity.** A nudge when driving, walking, or phone use doesn't fit their usual pattern. You won't need to review every drive.
- **Phone untouched for hours.** A heads-up when their phone hasn't been picked up in a long while.
- **Low battery.** A heads-up before their phone runs out of charge.
- **Arrivals that matter.** Know when they arrive at a hospital or ER, a police station, or an airport.
- **Hasn't left home in a while.** A gentle nudge when they've stayed home, or at another saved place, for 3 days straight.

### Optional alerts, if you need them

- **Heart rate.** Unusually high or low heart rate, available with Health & Fitness sharing.
- **Breathing rate.** An unusual breathing rate, also optional.
- **Loud places.** A heads-up when they're somewhere dangerously loud.
- **Unusual spending.** Unusually large spending on a linked bank account, with a threshold you set. Bank linking is optional.

**Note:** Every family is different. Mix and match the alerts that fit yours, and leave off the rest. You choose exactly which alerts you get about each Loved One, and you can turn alerts off for anyone with one tap. You can also review every alert about a Loved One from the past 24 hours. If you don't hear from Looop, everything looks normal, so you can rest easier.

## Activity, fitness, and health

> "Is Mom sleeping okay lately?"

**Subtitle:** Health & Fitness sharing is available and optional.

Activity is what they're doing: today's walking, driving, and resting time, and how much they've used their phone. With Health & Fitness sharing, you can also see their fitness (steps and workouts) and their health (sleep, including deep, REM, and light sleep, and naps; heart rate; and breathing rate).

Every number is marked Typical or Unusual, compared to what's normal for that person, so you can spot a change at a glance. Works with Apple Health and Apple Watch.

## Questions, answered

**How does Looop know when something's unusual?**
Looop runs on each family member's iPhone and learns their usual routine, like how much they walk, drive, and use their phone. If they choose, it also uses their Apple Watch, smart ring, or bank account. Looop compares each day to that person's own usual routine. When something is different, you get an alert.

**How often will I hear from Looop?**
Only when something changes. If you don't hear from Looop, everything looks normal. You choose which alerts you get for each person, and you can turn any of them off with one tap.

**Does my Loved One have to do anything?**
They install Looop on their iPhone, join your family group, and follow the setup steps on screen. After that, there's no new device to wear or charge, and nothing to fill in or remember.

**Does Looop track my family?**
No. Looop sends safety alerts instead of tracking. It uses the low-power location services built into iPhone, which wake the app when someone arrives somewhere important or moves, instead of keeping location running all day. There's a family map for when you need it, and you won't need to keep checking it.

**What does my Loved One share?**
What Looop needs to tell whether their day looks typical: things like whether they're driving, walking, or staying put, their phone battery, and the weather where they are. Family members in their Looop can also see where they are on the family map. Health & Fitness sharing is available if you need it. Before you invite anyone, walk through it together so there are no surprises.

**Is Health & Fitness sharing optional?**
Yes. Health & Fitness sharing is available and optional. It covers health details like heart rate and sleep, and fitness details like steps and workouts, and it can be turned off anytime.

**Does Looop sell my data?**
No. Looop never sells your data, and there are no ads, ever.

**Does Looop work outside the US?**
Yes! Severe weather alerts in more than 50 countries, including the US, Canada, Mexico, most of Europe, Israel, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, India, China, the Philippines, Brazil, South Africa, the UAE, and Singapore. Crime alerts are available in all 50 US states.

**Will Looop drain my battery?**
No. Looop is built to go easy on battery. It uses the low-power location services built into iPhone, which wake the app when someone arrives somewhere or moves a meaningful distance, instead of keeping location running all day. You also get a heads-up before a Loved One's phone runs low.

**How do I invite someone?**
Invite Loved Ones straight from your contacts. They can also join your group with a simple six-character code. If someone hasn't joined yet, you can send a friendly reminder.

**Does Looop detect emergencies?**
No. Looop gives you a heads-up when something seems off, so you know when to check in. If you think someone is in danger, call 911.

**Can I delete my data?**
Yes. You can leave a group anytime, and delete your account and data whenever you choose.

**How do I give feedback or ask for a new alert?**
Email the people who build Looop straight from the app, or send us a note through our Get in Touch form. We read everything.
