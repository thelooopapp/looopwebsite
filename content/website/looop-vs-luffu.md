## What Luffu does well

- **Built by Fitbit's founders** James Park and Eric Friedman started Fitbit and [announced Luffu](https://www.engadget.com/mobile/fitbit-founders-launch-luffu-a-way-to-integrate-your-familys-health-data-173251994.html) in February 2026. They have built wearables before.
- **Works with wearables you already own** Luffu's app reads Apple Health and Fitbit, and its site says it integrates with apps and wearables, so a family can start with what they have.
- **A shared health record** Medications, meals, medical records, and doctor visits, logged by voice, text, photo, or document, and shared with the parents, spouses, and caregivers you choose.
- **Answers in plain language** Ask about the family's health information and get answers, insights, and charts you can save and share.
- **A band that works without a phone nearby** Luffu Link has its own LTE and GPS, a help button for trusted contacts, a double-tap check-in, and up to five days of battery, in the US, expected early 2027.
- **iPhone and Android** Luffu's site lists iOS 18 and Android 14 and up.

## Where Looop and Luffu part ways

### Nothing to keep up with

Looop doesn't ask anyone to enter anything, and it keeps no medication list, care plan, or records. Once a Loved One joins, it speaks up when a day looks different from her usual. Quiet means all is well.

Luffu flags changes on its own too, and it goes further as a record. Its [home page](https://www.luffu.com/) describes logging by voice, text, photos, documents, or app integrations: medical records, medications, meals, and vital signs, with a plain-language Q&A over all of it. Its [day in the life](https://luffu.com/pages/life-with-luffu) page shows a Morning Brief, a lunch logged, a doctor visit with care plan updates, and a Family Retrospective at night. For a family managing a diagnosis, that record is the point.

### Alerts about her day, beyond her health

Looop tells you when a drive, a walk, or phone use looks unusual, when her phone sits untouched for hours, and when she arrives at a hospital, police station, or airport. These run on the iPhone she already carries.

Luffu's [home page](https://www.luffu.com/) centers its alerts on health: "missed medications, shifting health and activity patterns." Location sharing, check-ins, and a help button are listed on the [Luffu Link page](https://luffu.com/products/luffu-link), its band expected in early 2027. Whether the app alone offers them isn't stated on luffu.com.

### Storms and crime near Mom

Looop tells you when severe weather heads toward a Loved One, in more than 50 countries, and when crime is reported nearby, in all 50 US states, at the distance your family picks: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.

We didn't find weather or crime alerts on [luffu.com](https://www.luffu.com/) or the [Luffu Link page](https://luffu.com/products/luffu-link), checked November 6, 2026. In Looop, weather and crime are part of one daily answer about a Loved One's day: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

### A heads-up about an unusually large charge

If a Loved One links a bank account, Looop can let you know about unusually large spending, with a threshold you set. A sudden large payment can be an early sign of a scam, so you can call that same day. Bank linking is optional, runs through Plaid, and is never used for ads or sold.

We didn't find money or spending features on [luffu.com](https://www.luffu.com/), checked November 6, 2026.

### You can download Looop today

Looop launched on the App Store in May 2026. Download it, sign up with your phone number, and invite your family from your contacts.

Luffu [announced its app](https://www.engadget.com/mobile/fitbit-founders-launch-luffu-a-way-to-integrate-your-familys-health-data-173251994.html) on February 3, 2026, as a beta with a waitlist. Its [Luffu Link page](https://luffu.com/products/luffu-link) says the app will be available "later this year" and the band is "expected to ship early 2027."

### Help in an emergency

Looop puts 911 and up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One one tap away. It doesn't offer dispatch or crash detection, and it doesn't detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

Luffu's [Link page](https://luffu.com/products/luffu-link) answers "Does Luffu Link call 911 or emergency services?" with "No, it does not. However, it will contact trusted people that you specify via the Luffu app." The band's button sends help to those contacts, and a double tap lets others know she's okay.

### Advertisers

Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads and no sponsored offers in your notifications.

Luffu's [privacy notice](https://luffu.com/policies/privacy-policy), last updated August 23, 2026, says it does not "sell" consumer health data, as defined under state health privacy laws. The same notice says, "We may share your non-health personal information with third-party advertising partners." We quote both sentences so you can read them yourself.

## Where Luffu may fit better

- **Medications and doctor visits** If you're the one keeping up with prescriptions, meals, and care plans, Luffu is built to keep them in one place. Looop keeps no health record.
- **Questions about the family's health** If you want to ask about a medication's side effects and get an answer, Luffu offers that. Looop doesn't.
- **A parent who leaves the phone at home** Looop runs on the Loved One's iPhone. Luffu Link stays connected over LTE on its own, in the US, expected early 2027.
- **A help button on the wrist** If you want Mom to press a button and reach trusted contacts without her phone, that's what Luffu Link is for.
- **Android households** Looop is iPhone only today. Luffu lists iOS and Android.
- **The whole household, pets included** Luffu's <a class="text-link" href="https://www.techradar.com/health-fitness/fitness-trackers/were-building-something-i-want-for-my-family-and-your-family-fitbit-co-founders-are-launching-a-new-family-wide-health-tracking-app-but-we-have-our-reservations" rel="noopener">launch coverage</a> includes pets. Looop is only for people.

Choose Luffu if you want a shared family health record you keep by voice, text, photo, or document, answers to health questions, and a band with a help button for a parent who leaves the phone at home.

Choose Looop if you want health, fitness, and spending alerts plus severe weather and nearby crime, nothing to keep up with, an app on the App Store today, and your family's data never for sale.

## Questions families ask

**Is Looop or Luffu better for my family?**

Looop is built for families who care from a distance: parents living separately or a few states away, a relative living alone, kids at college, or siblings and friends who look out for each other. It tells you when to check in. Choose Luffu if you want a shared family health record you keep by voice, text, photo, or document, answers to health questions, and a band with a help button for a parent who leaves the phone at home. Choose Looop if you want health, fitness, and spending alerts plus severe weather and nearby crime, nothing to keep up with, an app on the App Store today, and your family's data never for sale.

**Does Looop help with Mom's medications?**

No. Looop keeps no medication list or records. If that's the job, Luffu is built for it.

**Does Mom need an Apple Watch for Looop?**

No. Looop works from her iPhone. Health & Fitness sharing is available if she wears an Apple Watch, Oura Ring, or Whoop, and it's her choice.

**Does Mom have to do anything each day?**

No. She accepts the invitation once. After that, Looop stays quiet when her day looks like her own normal and sends a heads-up when it doesn't.

**Does Luffu work without its band?**

Yes. Luffu's app reads Apple Health, Fitbit, and other apps and wearables, flags health and activity changes on its own, and keeps the records your family enters. Luffu Link adds its own health sensors, LTE so it works with no phone nearby in the US, a button for help messages to trusted contacts, a double-tap check-in, location sharing, and voice logging. The band is $299.99 ($249.99 on preorder), needs a Luffu Family Plan, and is expected to ship in early 2027.

**Can Looop or Luffu call 911 for us?**

Neither one calls 911 for you. Looop lets you call 911 in one tap and save up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One. Luffu Link sends help messages to the trusted contacts you set up, and Luffu says it doesn't call 911. Looop helps you know when to check in. It doesn't detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

**Does Looop send weather and crime alerts?**

Yes. Severe weather alerts work in more than 50 countries, and crime alerts work in all 50 US states, at the distance you choose: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.

**Does Looop work on Android?**

Not today. Looop is available for iPhone on the App Store. If your family uses Android phones, Luffu lists Android 14 and up.

**Does Looop sell my data?**

No. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads in the app and no sponsored offers in your notifications. You can delete your account and data whenever you choose.
