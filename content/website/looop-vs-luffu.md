## What Luffu does well

- **A shared health record** Medications, meals, medical records, vital signs, and doctor visits, added by voice, text, photo, or document, and shared with the parents, spouses, and caregivers you choose, with control over who can see or update what.
- **Alerts that run on their own** Luffu says it "watches for changes, missed medications, shifting health and activity patterns, and alerts you before they become concerns."
- **Answers in plain language** Ask about the family's health information and get answers, insights, and charts you can adjust, save, and share.
- **A band that works without a phone nearby** Luffu Link has its own LTE and GPS, a help button for trusted contacts, a double-tap check-in, and up to five days of battery, in the US, expected early 2027.
- **iPhone and Android** Luffu's site lists iOS 18 and Android 14 and up.

## Where Looop and Luffu part ways

### The whole of their day, in one answer

Looop takes a holistic approach to family care. Each day, severe weather, nearby crime, an unusually large charge, health and fitness readings, and the shape of the day come together in one answer about each Loved One: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call. It's all in one place, so you're not checking five apps to piece together how everyone's doing.

Luffu's [home page](https://www.luffu.com/) describes a different center: "stay on top of your whole family's health, safety, and wellbeing," with alerts for "missed medications, shifting health and activity patterns." Its [day in the life](https://luffu.com/pages/life-with-luffu) page shows a morning summary, a doctor visit with care plan updates, a question about a medication's side effects, and an evening recap. For a family managing a diagnosis, that depth is the point.

### Looop is automatic

A Loved One accepts an invitation once. After that, Looop is automatic: quiet when a day looks like their own normal, and a heads-up when it doesn't. There's no record to keep.

Luffu is built around a record your family keeps. Its [home page](https://www.luffu.com/) describes adding medications, meals, medical records, and vital signs by voice, text, photo, or document, then asking questions about what's there. For a family managing a diagnosis, that record is the point. For a family that wants to know everyone's okay, it's one more thing to do.

### Severe weather and nearby crime

Looop tells you when severe weather is heading toward a Loved One, in more than 50 countries, and when crime is reported nearby, in all 50 US states, at the distance your family picks: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles. Instead of checking the weather in five cities, families get one heads-up when it matters.

We didn't find weather or crime alerts on [luffu.com](https://www.luffu.com/) or the [Luffu Link page](https://luffu.com/products/luffu-link), checked November 6, 2026. Luffu Link lists location sharing, notifications, and help and check-in messages, and the band is expected in early 2027.

### A heads-up about an unusually large charge

If a Loved One links a bank account, Looop can let you know about unusually large spending, with a threshold you set. A sudden large payment can be an early sign of a scam, so you can call that same day. Bank linking is optional, runs through Plaid, and is never used for ads or sold.

We didn't find money or spending features on [luffu.com](https://www.luffu.com/), checked November 6, 2026. Luffu's day-in-the-life page shows to-dos, meals, and a dinner plan, and its Link page covers health, location, and help messages.

### Help in an emergency

Neither app calls 911 for you. Looop puts 911 and up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One one tap away, on the iPhone they already carry. It doesn't offer dispatch or crash detection, and it doesn't detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

Luffu's [Link page](https://luffu.com/products/luffu-link) answers "Does Luffu Link call 911 or emergency services?" with "No, it does not. However, it will contact trusted people that you specify via the Luffu app." The band's button sends help to those contacts, and a double tap lets family know you're okay. Those features need cellular coverage and work only in the US, per the same page.

### Advertisers and your data

Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads and no sponsored offers in your notifications. Your family's data isn't for sale.

Luffu's [privacy notice](https://luffu.com/policies/privacy-policy), last updated August 23, 2026, says it does not "sell" consumer health data, as defined under state health privacy laws. The same notice says, "We may share your non-health personal information with third-party advertising partners." It covers Luffu's website and mobile app. We quote both sentences so you can read them yourself.

### You can download Looop today

Looop launched on the App Store in May 2026. Download it, sign up with your phone number, and invite your family from your contacts.

Luffu [announced its app](https://www.engadget.com/mobile/fitbit-founders-launch-luffu-a-way-to-integrate-your-familys-health-data-173251994.html) on February 3, 2026, as a beta with a waitlist. We found no Luffu app on the App Store on November 6, 2026. Its [Luffu Link page](https://luffu.com/products/luffu-link) says the app will be available "later this year" and the band is "expected to ship early 2027."

## Where Luffu may fit better

- **Medications, records, and doctor visits** If someone in your family keeps up with prescriptions, care plans, and medical records for everyone, Luffu is built to keep them in one place. Looop keeps no health record.
- **Questions about the family's health** If you want to ask about a medication's side effects and get an answer, Luffu offers that. Looop doesn't.
- **One person managing everyone's information** Luffu says you can "manage everything on behalf of your family," and control what each person can see or update.
- **A parent who leaves the phone at home** Looop runs on the Loved One's iPhone. Luffu Link stays connected over its own LTE in the US, expected early 2027.
- **A help button on the wrist** If you want a parent to press a button and reach trusted contacts without a phone nearby, that's what Luffu Link is for.
- **Android households** Looop is iPhone only today. Luffu lists iOS and Android.

Choose Luffu if you want a shared family health record your family adds to by voice, text, photo, or document, answers to health questions in plain language, and a band with a help button for a parent who leaves the phone at home.

Choose Looop if you want one daily answer about each person you love that covers weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, and activity, automatic alerts, an app on the App Store today, and your family's data never for sale.

## Questions families ask

**Is Looop or Luffu better for our family?**

Looop is built for families who care from a distance: parents a few states away, a relative living alone, kids at college, or siblings and friends who look out for each other. It tells you when to check in, about the whole of a day. Luffu is built for the caregiver who keeps up with the family's medications, records, and doctor visits in one place, with alerts when health or activity changes. If your family needs a health record, Luffu is built for that. If you want one automatic daily answer, that's Looop.

**Can one Looop cover a parent in another state and a kid at college?**

Yes. Each Loved One gets their own daily answer, based on their own routine. A storm heading toward your parents' town and a crime report near a campus show up in the same app, each at the distance you choose.

**Does Looop help with medications?**

No. Looop keeps no medication list, care plan, or records. If that's the job, Luffu is built for it.

**Does anyone in our family have to do anything each day?**

No. Each Loved One accepts an invitation once. After that, Looop is automatic. Say your dad usually drives to golf on Tuesdays and this week his phone hasn't moved by noon. That's the kind of day Looop would flag.

**Do our parents need an Apple Watch for Looop?**

No. Looop works from the iPhone they already carry. Health & Fitness sharing is available if they wear an Apple Watch, Oura Ring, or Whoop, and it's their choice.

**Does Luffu work without its band?**

Yes. Luffu's app reads Apple Health and Fitbit, flags health and activity changes on its own, and keeps the records your family adds. Luffu Link adds its own health sensors, LTE so it works with no phone nearby in the US, a button for help messages to trusted contacts, a double-tap check-in, location sharing, and voice entry. The band is $299.99 ($249.99 on preorder), needs a Luffu Family Plan, and is expected to ship in early 2027.

**Can Looop or Luffu call 911 for us?**

Neither one calls 911 for you. Looop lets you call 911 in one tap and save up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One. Luffu Link sends help messages to the trusted contacts you set up, and Luffu says it doesn't call 911. Looop helps you know when to check in. It doesn't detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

**Does Looop send weather and crime alerts?**

Yes. Severe weather alerts work in more than 50 countries, and crime alerts work in all 50 US states, at the distance you choose: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.

**Does Looop work on Android?**

Not today. Looop is available for iPhone on the App Store. If your family uses Android phones, Luffu lists Android 14 and up.

**Does Looop sell our data?**

No. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads in the app and no sponsored offers in your notifications. You can delete your account and data whenever you choose.
