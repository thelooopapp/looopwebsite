## "Did she get home okay?"

**Subtitle:** Calm alerts, so you know when to reach out.

The rest of the time, Looop stays quiet.

- **Crime alerts near campus.** A heads-up when something is reported nearby, in all 50 US states.
- **Severe weather.** Storms, flood warnings, and extreme heat or cold near their school, during finals week or any week.
- **Wherever they go.** If they're on spring break in Mexico or a semester abroad in Spain or Japan, you'll still hear about severe weather near them, in more than 50 countries. Crime alerts work anywhere in the US.
- **Unusual Day.** A gentle nudge when their day looks very different from their own normal.
- **Low battery.** Know when their phone is about to run out of charge, so a missed text doesn't send you spiraling.
- **Airport arrivals.** Know when they land for winter break.

An alert leads to one calm text, so you can skip the ten anxious ones.

## Twins in two states? Parents in a third?

**Subtitle:** One app for everyone you love.

Plenty of parents are worried about college kids and their own parents at the same time. Add everyone to one Looop. You'll see who is having a typical day at a glance, and get one heads-up when someone needs a call.

Looop is made for grown kids living on their own, and it works alongside Find My if you already share locations. [See how family safety apps compare](compare/index.html)

## A word for your student

> "I moved out. Please trust me."

**Subtitle:** Your parents stop texting every hour.  
You keep living your life.

Looop only speaks up when something seems off, like a storm near campus or a day that looks really unusual for you. Your parents don't get a play-by-play of your day. It sends alerts instead of tracking you, with no ads and nothing sold.

- **It's an invite.** You decide whether to join.
- **Health & Fitness sharing is available.** It's optional, so add it if you need it.
- **You can leave a group anytime.**
- **It works both ways.** Want a heads-up if a storm hits back home? You get that too.

## Make it a conversation

Before move-in day, talk about what Looop shares and why. Agree on what a check-in looks like. Maybe a quick "you okay?" text after an alert, and the usual Sunday call for everything else.
