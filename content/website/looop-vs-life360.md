# Looop vs. Life360

**Subtitle:** Two different ideas of family safety.

Looop is a safety notifications app. It tells you when to check in, so the people you love can live their lives. It never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers.

If you're looking for a Life360 alternative without tracking, here's how the two compare, with sources.

[Download Looop]

## What Looop is

- **Notifications, not tracking.** Alerts about nearby crime, severe weather and unusual activity.
- **Just enough to know.** Looop uses only what it needs to tell whether something unusual is going on.
- **Independence, protected.** Loved ones live their day. Looop only speaks up when something looks off.
- **Check in when it counts.** No constant calls. Just a nudge when it's worth reaching out.
- **Your data is never for sale.** No data sales. No advertisers.

## Side by side

| | Life360 | Looop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A GPS location tracking app: a live family map plus location history | A safety notifications app: alerts that tell you when to check in |
| Location | Precise, continuous location; its own setup guide cites accuracy to about 50 feet | Just enough to know if something unusual is going on, never precise tracking |
| What you get notified about | Arrivals, departures and missed arrivals at set places | Nearby crime, severe weather, and unusual driving, walking or phone activity |
| Independence | Built around watching where family members are and where they've been | Loved ones live their day; Looop only speaks up when something looks off |
| Check-ins | Encourages looking at the map to see where everyone is | Nudges you to check in only when it's warranted, so you don't bother anyone needlessly |
| Advertising | Runs an ad business built on its members' location data; Uber is a key partner | No advertisers. Ever. |
| Your data | Has a history of selling its members' location data to a dozen data brokers, including Cuebiq, X-Mode, SafeGraph and Allstate's Arity, and still sells location data today | Never sold |

## The facts behind the comparison

- **Uber in your notifications.** Life360 launched its ad platform with Uber as a brand partner. Members who land at an airport get an Uber ride offer as a push notification; Life360 reports 34 million of these were sent. Sources: [Life360 press release](https://www.life360.com/en-us/press/life360-unveils-advertising-platform-to-connect-brands-with-its-engaged-community-of-40-million-u-s-monthly-active-users), [Life360 investor release (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1581760/000158176025000225/a11102025ex992investorpr.htm)
- **Place Ads.** In June 2025 Life360 launched Place Ads, which target families based on the real-world places they visit. Source: [Life360 press release](https://www.life360.com/press/life360-enhances-ad-platform-launch-place-ads-and-uplift)
- **Ads beyond the app.** Life360 bought the ad platform Nativo to serve ads using its first-party family data across a publisher network, and reported nearly $20 million in ad revenue in Q1 2026, up over 300% year over year. Adweek reported a LiveRamp partnership that lets advertisers target Life360 users off-site. Sources: [Life360 Q1 2026 release (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001581760/000158176026000079/a992-q126life360_arinves.htm), [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/exclusive-location-app-life360-with-help-from-brands-like-uber-is-growing-a-10m-ad-business-this-year/)
- **Data sales.** The Markup reported in 2021 that Life360 sold precise location data to brokers including Cuebiq, X-Mode, SafeGraph and Arity. Life360 then stopped most of those sales but kept Arity and began selling aggregated data to Placer.ai, which it describes as a growing data business. Sources: [The Markup](https://themarkup.org/privacy/2021/12/06/the-popular-family-safety-app-life360-is-selling-precise-location-data-on-its-tens-of-millions-of-user), [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/22/01/28/life360-to-stop-selling-precise-location-data-of-users), [Life360 Q3 2024 release (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1581760/000158176024000105/life360incq324mediarelea.htm)

Information about Life360 is drawn from the public sources linked above, as checked on October 5, 2026. Life360 is a trademark of its owner. Looop is not affiliated with Life360.
