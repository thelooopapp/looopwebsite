# What Does "Data Used to Track You" Mean on the App Store?

"Data Used to Track You" is one section of an app's privacy label on the App Store. It lists the kinds of data the developer says may be used for tracking, in Apple's sense of the word.

Apple defines it this way: "Tracking occurs when data collected about you or your device is linked with third-party data for targeted advertising or advertising measurement purposes." Apple adds, "Tracking can also occur when data about you or your device is shared with a data broker."

## What Apple means by third-party data

Apple describes third-party data as "any data about you or your device collected from apps, websites, or offline properties not owned by the developer." So tracking, in Apple's terms, is about connecting what one app knows with data from other companies, for ads or ad measurement, or sharing it with a data broker.

Apple also lists two cases that don't count: data "linked solely on your device" that isn't sent off it in a way that can identify you, and data shared with a data broker "solely for fraud detection and prevention" or security, on the developer's behalf.

## The other sections of the label

- **Data Linked to You:** "Data that is linked to your identity (via your account, device, or other details)."
- **Data Not Linked to You:** "Data that is not linked to your identity (via your account, device, or other details)."

Under each section, the label lists types of data, like location or contact info.

## How to read a label

Open any app's App Store page and scroll to App Privacy. Each label starts with a line saying the developer "indicated that the app's privacy practices may include handling of data as described below." The label is the developer's own description of its practices.

A few things to look for:

- Whether there's a "Data Used to Track You" section at all, and which data types are in it.
- Which data is linked to you, and for what purpose.
- Whether the purposes include anything beyond running the app.

## Questions families ask

### Does "Data Used to Track You" mean an app sells my data?

Not necessarily. In Apple's definition, tracking means linking data about you or your device with third-party data for targeted ads or ad measurement, or sharing it with a data broker. The label doesn't say which of these an app does.

### Who fills in the privacy label?

The developer. Each label says the developer "indicated that the app's privacy practices may include handling of data as described below."

### Where do I find an app's privacy label?

On the app's App Store page, in the App Privacy section.
