## Who each app is best for

**Question:** "What is each one good at?"
**Subtitle:** Each one is good at something. Here's who each fits.

### Life360: Best for a live map with emergency help

Life360 is one of the best-known family apps. It's built around a live family map with location history, place alerts, and driving reports, and it works on iPhone and Android. Paid plans add crash detection, and Gold and Platinum include SOS with 24/7 emergency dispatch and roadside help.

It does more than location now. Severe weather alerts come through AccuWeather. Its [Safety Incidents](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/38226266501911-Safety-Incidents) feature, new in 2026, puts recent, high-severity events within one mile on the map, with a notification. Its free [Subscription Tracker](https://www.life360.com/blog/subscription-tracker) links bank accounts through Plaid to show recurring charges across a Circle.

Life360 also runs a [growing ad business](https://investors.life360.com/static-files/e6bd1845-9bc7-429b-aada-fd260b7fc517). By connecting an account to Subscription Tracker, you agree that "Life360 may use your transaction data to show you relevant ads and measure ad performance," according to its [setup page](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/39739802477591-Set-Up-Subscription-Tracker). Life360 has a history of selling location data to a dozen data brokers.

**Choose Life360 if** you want to pay more for precision GPS tracking, driving details recorded and stored, call center support, targeted ads, and your family's data shared with its partners.

### iSharing: Best for a family map with inactivity alerts

iSharing, from iSharingSoft in Irvine, California, shares real-time location on a map with Street View. It offers place alerts for home, work, and school, location history from 7 days to a year depending on plan, a panic or SOS alert with recorded sound, messaging, and temporary group sharing called Meetups.

It also reaches out to families with older parents. Its "Elderly Care" alerts let you know when someone hasn't used their phone, on Silver plans and up, and Gold and up adds driving reports. Premium plans are listed from $7.99 a month or $69.99 a year on the App Store, with a seven-day free trial.

On data, iSharing's [privacy policy](https://isharingsoft.com/privacy_policy), last revised May 21, 2026, says: "We may also share location information with third parties for advertising, research, and analytic purposes." Its [App Store label](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id416436167) lists seven kinds of data under "Data Used to Track You."

**Best for:** families who want a map with place alerts, a panic button, and inactivity alerts at a lower monthly price than Life360's top plans, and who are comfortable with its data policy.

### Looop: Best for caring from a distance

Looop is our app, so read this with that in mind. It's a safety notifications app from LooopApp LLC, an independent Austin startup that launched in May 2026. Most family apps are built around a map: they answer "Where is she?" Looop answers "Is Mom okay today?"

Looop takes a holistic approach to family care: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day. You get a heads-up when severe weather heads toward a Loved One, in more than 50 countries, and when crime is reported nearby, in all 50 US states, at 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles. If driving, walking, or phone activity looks unusual, you get a gentle nudge. Spending alerts and Health & Fitness sharing through Apple Health are available if you need them.

Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call. There's a family map for when you need it, for example after a nudge to check in.

Looop has no ads, ever, and your family's data is never for sale. It's iPhone only, and it isn't an emergency service. It doesn't send help or detect crashes, though 911 and up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One are one tap away.

**Choose Looop if** you want safety alerts instead of tracking, a nudge when a drive looks unusual, no ads ever, and your family's data never for sale. Peace of mind for you, independence for them.

## Where Looop is different

**Question:** "So what's actually different?"
**Subtitle:** Life360 and iSharing answer "Where is she?" Looop answers "Is Mom okay today?"

### All in one place, in one calm answer

No more checking the weather in five cities, a map in one app, and bank alerts in another. It's all in one place.

A quiet phone is one signal. Looop looks at the whole day: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day, in one answer.

### Alerts instead of a map

Looop stays quiet when all is well and sends a heads-up when something looks off. You don't have to keep checking a screen, and the people you love don't get a "where are you?" text every evening.

### Built for parents and the people who love them

Looop is made for life after the driving years: a parent a few states away, kids at college, a sibling or friend who lives alone. Loved Ones join by invitation and keep their routine. There are no sessions or timers for them to start.

### No ads, and your family's data is never for sale

Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. Looop's App Store privacy label has no "Data Used to Track You" section, and the listing doesn't say it contains advertising.

Spending alerts run through Plaid, and that data is never used for ads or sold.

## Questions families ask

### Is Life360 or iSharing better?

It depends on what you need. Life360 has more emergency features, like crash detection on paid plans and 24/7 emergency dispatch on Gold and Platinum. iSharing's premium plans are listed at lower prices and include inactivity alerts. Both are built around a live map. If you'd rather get a heads-up than watch a map, Looop is a different option.

### Does iSharing work for checking on an aging parent?

iSharing offers "Elderly Care" alerts when someone hasn't used their phone, plus place alerts and SOS. A quiet phone is one signal. Looop looks at the whole day, including weather, crime, and unusual activity, and gives you one answer. [Read more about iSharing for aging parents](../answers/isharing-for-elderly-parents.html).

### Can I use Looop alongside Life360 or iSharing?

Yes. Looop doesn't replace a map app if your family wants one. Some families keep a map for the kids and use Looop for the people they care for from a distance.

### Does Looop have SOS or crash detection?

No. Looop doesn't have crash detection, a dispatch service, or roadside help. It lets you call 911 in one tap and save up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One. Looop helps you know when to check in, and it doesn't detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

### Does Looop show ads or sell my data?

No. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads in the app and no sponsored offers in your notifications.
