## What each app does well, and where it may fit better

**Question:** "Who is each one for?"

### What Life360 does well

Life360 is one of the best-known family apps. It's built around a live family map with location history, place alerts, and driving reports, and it works on iPhone and Android. Paid plans add crash detection, and Gold and Platinum include SOS with 24/7 emergency dispatch and roadside help.

And it now goes beyond location. Severe weather alerts come through AccuWeather. Its [Safety Incidents](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/38226266501911-Safety-Incidents) feature, new in 2026, puts recent, high-severity events within one mile on the map, with a notification. Its free [Subscription Tracker](https://www.life360.com/blog/subscription-tracker) links bank accounts through Plaid to show recurring charges across a Circle.

On data, Life360 runs a [growing ad business](https://investors.life360.com/static-files/e6bd1845-9bc7-429b-aada-fd260b7fc517). Its [setup page](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/39739802477591-Set-Up-Subscription-Tracker) says that by connecting an account to Subscription Tracker, you agree that "Life360 may use your transaction data to show you relevant ads and measure ad performance in the Life360 app and elsewhere." Life360 has a history of selling location data to a dozen data brokers.

**Where Life360 may fit better:** families with Android phones or a new teen driver, and anyone who wants crash detection on paid plans and 24/7 emergency dispatch on Gold and Platinum.

**Choose Life360 if** you want to pay more for precision GPS tracking, driving details recorded and stored, call center support, targeted ads, and your family's data being shared with its partners.

### What Glympse does well

Glympse, from Glympse Inc., is built for temporary sharing. You set how long to share, send a link, and the share expires automatically. The people you send it to don't need a login or an account. You can also create invite-only Private Groups for people you trust.

Glympse isn't built as a daily safety app. Nothing stays on once the timer runs out. Premium costs $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glympse-share-your-location/id330316698). The listing says it contains advertising, and its description says, "Your data is never sold." Its privacy label lists usage data under "Data Used to Track You."

**Where Glympse may fit better:** friends, partners, and families who want to share a drive home, a trip, or a meetup for a set time, without an always-on connection.

### Looop: Best for looking out for each other every day

Looop is our own app, so read this part with that in mind. It's a safety notifications app from LooopApp LLC, an independent Austin startup that launched in May 2026.

Looop takes a holistic approach to family care: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day. You get a heads-up when severe weather heads toward a Loved One, in more than 50 countries, and when crime is reported nearby, in all 50 US states, at 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles. If driving, walking, or phone activity looks unusual, you get a gentle nudge. Spending alerts and Health & Fitness sharing through Apple Health are available if you need them.

Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call. There's a family map, too, for when a nudge makes you want to check.

Looop has no ads, ever, and your family's data is never for sale. It's iPhone only, and it isn't an emergency service. It doesn't send help or detect crashes, though 911 and up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One are one tap away.

**Choose Looop if** you want safety alerts instead of tracking, a nudge when a drive looks unusual, no ads ever, and your family's data never for sale. Peace of mind for you, independence for them.

## What Looop does without a link

**Question:** "Couldn't we just send a link when it matters?"

### Everyone's day, on one calm screen

See everyone you care about on one calm screen, and know at a glance who's okay.

Looop brings weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day together in one answer, so no one has to remember to send a link.

### Quiet unless something looks off

You won't be refreshing a screen, and nobody gets a "where are you?" text every evening. Looop sends a heads-up only when something looks off.

### For anyone you look out for

Looop is made for life after the driving years: a parent a few states away, kids at college, a sibling or friend who lives alone. Loved Ones join by invitation and keep their routine. There are no sessions or timers for them to start.

Spending alerts are optional. They go through Plaid, and that data is never used for ads or sold.

## Questions about Glympse, Life360, and Looop

### Is Life360 or Glympse better?

Each does a different job. Life360 is an always-on family map with place alerts, driving reports, and emergency features on paid plans. Glympse shares your location for a set time, with a link that expires on its own. Looop tells you when someone you look out for has a day that looks unusual.

### Is Glympse a family safety app?

Glympse is built for temporary sharing, like a drive home or a meetup. It isn't built around ongoing alerts, and shares end when the time runs out.

### Can friends use Looop to look out for each other?

Yes. Looop works for friends, siblings, and partners as well as parents. Everyone joins by invitation, and you each get a heads-up if something seems off, like severe weather or a night that looks unusual.

### Does Looop have SOS or crash detection?

No. Looop doesn't detect crashes or dispatch help, and it has no roadside service. 911 is one tap away in the app, and you can save up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One. Looop is there to tell you when to check in, and it doesn't detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

### Does Looop show ads or sell my data?

No. You won't see ads in Looop or sponsored offers in your notifications. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers.
