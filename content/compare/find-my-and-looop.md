## What Apple's tools do well

**Subtitle:** They're free, they're built in, and millions of families already use them.

### Free and already there

Find My comes on every iPhone. If Mom has one, there's nothing to buy or download.

### Phones, keys, and AirPods

Find My finds lost Apple devices and AirTags, and you can share an AirTag with up to five people.

### Help with no signal

On iPhone 14 and later, Find My can share location over satellite when there's no cell service or Wi-Fi.

### Check In for one trip

Check In in Messages lets someone tell a friend or family member they got home, for a walk home or a drive across town.

### Health Sharing

In the Health app, a person can share Health data with family.

### Privacy you can check

Find My's App Store label has no "Data Used to Track You" section.

## Where Looop is different

**Question:** "So what does Looop add?"
**Subtitle:** Looop works alongside Find My. Here's what it adds.

### All in one place

Apple's tools live in separate places. Looop brings it all together in one place: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of her day.

No more checking the weather in five cities, a map in one app, and bank alerts in another. Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

### Alerts instead of a map

Find My is there when you open it and ask "Where is she?" Looop answers "Is Mom okay today?" and speaks up on its own: a storm heading her way, crime reported nearby, or a day that looks unusual.

### Built for parents and the people who love them

Looop is built for adult children caring from a distance, and for siblings who want the same heads-up. See everyone you care about on one calm screen, and know at a glance who's okay.

### Nothing for Mom to start

Check In works when the person starts a session. Looop works quietly in the background once she's joined, so there are no sessions, timers, or check-ins for her to remember. She lives her day.

### Your family's data isn't for sale

Like Find My, Looop's App Store privacy label has no "Data Used to Track You" section. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers.

Looop isn't an emergency service. It doesn't call 911 for you, send help, or detect falls or crashes. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

## How families use both

**Question:** "How would we use both?"
**Subtitle:** Keep what works. Add the heads-up.

### Keep Find My for things and trips

Mom keeps sharing her location in Find My, finding her phone, and using Check In for a late drive home, just as she does now.

### Add Looop for the day

Looop sends you one heads-up when a storm heads toward her, when crime is reported near her, or when her day looks unusual. Quiet means all is well.

### Then reach out

## Where Find My may be enough

**Question:** "Do we need Looop at all?"
**Subtitle:** We want you to pick what fits your family.

### You just want to see where she is

If a location on a map answers your question, Find My does that for free.

### Lost phones and keys

Find My finds devices and AirTags. Looop doesn't help you find things.

### Places with no signal

Satellite location sharing on newer iPhones covers trips beyond cell service. Looop needs a connection.

### One trip at a time

If you mostly worry about one drive or one walk home, Check In may be all you need.

## Questions families ask

**Do I need Looop if Mom already shares her location in Find My?**
Find My answers "Where is she?" Looop answers "Is Mom okay today?" It tells you when a storm heads her way, when crime is reported nearby, or when her day looks unusual, so you know when to call. Many families use both.

**Can I use Find My and Looop together?**
Yes. Looop works alongside Find My. Keep Find My for her phone, her AirTags, and sharing location. Looop runs on its own and adds the heads-up about her day.

**Does Apple's Check In work for checking on a parent?**
Check In works well for one walk or one drive. The person being checked on starts each session in Messages, with a timer or a destination. Looop doesn't need Mom to start anything. [Read the full answer](../answers/apple-check-in-for-parents.html).

**Does Looop work with Apple Watch and Apple Health?**
Yes. If your Loved One wears an Apple Watch, Oura Ring, or Whoop, Health & Fitness sharing through Apple Health is available if you need it. Nothing new to install on the watch.

**Is Looop an emergency service?**
Looop isn't an emergency service. It doesn't call 911 for you, send help, or detect falls or crashes. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.
