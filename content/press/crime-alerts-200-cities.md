# Looop Expands Crime Alerts to All 50 US States, Adding to Its Alerts for Aging Parents

## Families who already get alerts about a parent's health, fitness, and spending will now also hear when crime is reported near them, anywhere in the US

**AUSTIN, Texas, Oct. 28, 2026:** Looop, the safety notifications app for families who care from a distance, today expanded its crime alerts to all 50 US states. Families already get alerts about a parent's health, fitness, and unusual spending, along with severe weather and changes in her routine. Now they also hear when crime is reported nearby, wherever they go, at a distance they choose: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.

Crime alerts work wherever a loved one goes in the US: a winter at the vacation home in Florida, a wine weekend in Napa, a college road trip, or a cruise that leaves from Galveston.

For a family spread across several states, local news rarely travels. An adult child in Denver may never hear about an incident a few blocks from Mom's house in Columbus. A parent may not learn about a robbery near campus until their daughter mentions it days later. With Looop, that news reaches the family in a notification, with no crime map to scroll.

"The first thing most of us want after hearing about a crime near Mom's house is to call and hear her voice. Now that we're in all 50 states, every family in the US can get that heads-up," said the team behind Looop.

**How Nearby Crime Alerts work**

- **Near the people you love.** Alerts only cover reported crime close to each loved one.
- **A distance each family chooses.** Families set the alert distance for each loved one: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.
- **Alerts on your terms.** Families choose exactly which alerts they get about each person, and can turn any alert off with one tap.
- **One-tap check-in.** Call, text, or FaceTime straight from a loved one's card, or send a quick "Are you ok?"

Looop is not an emergency service and does not prevent crime. In an emergency, families should call 911.

**One answer about each loved one**

Looop expanded its crime alerts as one part of a holistic approach to family care. It brings together health, fitness, spending, activity, weather, crime, and the shape of each day, and turns it into one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call. Every loved one joins by invitation and keeps their independence. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers.

Some family apps now show crime as pins on a map. With Looop, families choose the distance (1, 3, 5, or 10 miles) and crime is one part of a daily answer about each loved one.
