# A Reason to Call Mom: Looop's Spending Alerts Flag Unusually Large Charges

## An optional heads-up about an unusually large charge on a linked account gives families a reason to call the same day

**AUSTIN, Texas, Oct. 29, 2026:** Looop, the safety notifications app for families who care from a distance, is reminding families about its spending alerts as Medicare open enrollment runs through December 7 and the holiday shopping season begins. When a loved one chooses to link a bank account, Looop can send the family a heads-up about unusually large spending, based on a threshold the family sets. The alert gives families a reason to call that day and ask. A sudden large payment is often perfectly normal, but it can be an early sign of a scam.

Scams that reach older adults often move fast. The FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center reports that people 60 and older filed 201,266 complaints in 2025 and reported $7.748 billion in losses, up 59% from 2024 ([2025 IC3 Annual Report](https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf)). The Federal Trade Commission says older adults report the highest total losses to fraud paid by bank transfer, and that scammers often press people to move money to "protect it" ([Protecting Older Consumers, 2024-2025](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/P144400-OlderAdultsReportDec2025.pdf)). A family that hears about an unusual payment early has a better chance to talk before more money moves.

"Scams work by getting someone to act alone and fast. A heads-up about an unusual payment gives the family a reason to call Mom that afternoon and ask, 'Hey, was that you?' Usually she'll say yes and tell you about the new fridge. If she's unsure, the two of you can call the bank together," said the team behind Looop.

**How spending alerts work**

- **Optional bank linking.** Bank linking is optional and runs through Plaid. A loved one decides whether to link an account.
- **A threshold the family sets.** Families choose what counts as unusually large, so small, everyday purchases stay quiet.
- **A gentle heads-up.** A large payment is often perfectly normal, so the alert only suggests it might be a good time to check in.
- **Alerts on your terms.** Families choose exactly which alerts they get about each loved one and can turn any alert off with one tap.
- **One-tap check-in.** Call, text, or FaceTime straight from a loved one's card.

Looop does not detect or prevent scams, and it does not reimburse losses. If a family suspects a scam, they should contact the bank right away and report it to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and to the FBI at ic3.gov.

**A reason to talk the same day**

Some family safety plans focus on what happens after money is gone. Life360, for example, lists identity theft protection and stolen funds reimbursement of up to $25,000 on its Gold plan and up to $1 million on its Platinum plan ([Life360 on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/life360-family-safety-gps/id384830320), as of Oct. 2026), and said in September 2026 that it is working on scam protection for aging parents ([Goldman Sachs conference coverage](https://in.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/life360-at-goldman-sachs-conference-growth-broadens-beyond-family-tracking-93CH-5589446)). Looop's alert is simply a reason to call and talk the same day.

Life360 also offers a free Subscription Tracker, which links checking accounts and credit cards through Plaid and watches recurring charges, price changes, and renewals ([Life360 blog](https://www.life360.com/blog/subscription-tracker), May 2026). By connecting an account, members agree that "Life360 may use your transaction data to show you relevant ads and measure ad performance in the Life360 app and elsewhere." That's from Life360's [Set Up Subscription Tracker](https://support.life360.com/hc/en-us/articles/39739802477591-Set-Up-Subscription-Tracker) page, checked Oct. 26, 2026. Looop's spending alerts also run through Plaid. They flag an unusually large charge, and that data is never used for ads or sold.

Spending alerts are one part of Looop's holistic approach to family care. Looop brings together weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day, and turns it into one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

**Private by design**

Spending data is sensitive. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. Every loved one joins by invitation and chooses what to share.
