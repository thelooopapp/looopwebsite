Looop Pitch Deck

1 of 10

Is Everyone Okay Today?

My family of five lives in five different cities. Every day I wonder: Is everyone okay today? Looop answers that, once a day, from the phones they already have.

Jeff Carpenter, founder · Austin, Texas · jeff@thelooopapp.com

2 of 10

Distributed Families Are Hard to Keep Up With

  • My parents live alone, in separate homes, and my twins are at college in two states.
  • Keeping up means checking the weather in five cities, a map in one app, and crime alerts in another.
  • During the July 2025 Hill Country flood, I waited until that night to hear my twins were safe at their camps.
  • 63 million Americans are family caregivers, up 45% in a decade, and 64% report high emotional stress (AARP/NAC 2025).

3 of 10

One Daily Answer About Each Loved One

Looop sends alerts about health, fitness, spending, activity, weather, crime, and the shape of each day. Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: A Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

4 of 10

The Leaders Are Moving Here

  • Life360: 102.4 million monthly users and 3.2 million Paying Circles. It has named aging parents as a next line of business.
  • Fitbit's founders sold Fitbit to Google for $2.1 billion, then launched Luffu for the same families. Axios reported it's self-funded, and its band ships in early 2027.
  • Looop works with iPhone, Apple Watch, and any device that shares with Apple Health, with nothing new to buy or wear.

5 of 10

One Easy Answer Each Day

  • Looop learns each loved one's own patterns, then turns weather, crime, spending, health, and activity into one answer: A Typical Day or an Unusual Day.
  • Alerts are measured against that person's normal, so families hear what's actually different.
  • Reliability made Life360 the leader. Looop keeps working in the background, after a force-close, after a restart, and right after a dead battery is charged.

Looop never sells data and doesn't work with advertisers.

6 of 10

Live and Ready to Grow

  • Live on iPhone since May 2026, with early families as our usability and UI testers.
  • 417 product updates shipped since March 30, 2026.
  • A June 2026 usability test: 78% finished setup, and we found and fixed the invite gap. Sign-up now requires an accepted invite.
  • Pre-marketing and pre-revenue, with Looop Plus and the free trial live.

7 of 10

Anyone Can View for Free

  • Anyone in the family can view everyone's status for free. Each person who wants push alerts subscribes to Looop Plus: $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year.
  • 63 million US family caregivers at $49.99 a year is about $3.2 billion a year.
  • Every loved one joins by invitation, so each family brings in more people.

8 of 10

Built for Distributed Families

  • Life360 does a lot well: A live family map, driving safety, crash detection, and weather and crime alerts. It's built around where everyone is.
  • Luffu pairs an app with a $299.99 band and a $19.99 monthly plan, shipping early 2027. Looop works with the phone and watch people already have.
  • Looop works alongside Find My.
  • Medical alert services answer emergencies. Looop isn't an emergency service; it tells you when a call might be worth it.

9 of 10

Jeff's Fourth Startup

  • Jeff Carpenter, solo founder, Austin. Looop is his fourth startup, after AgileGraph, Ringtail Design, and Live Earth.
  • Bootstrapped Ringtail to 50 people and $7M+ in revenue. As CEO of Live Earth, signed 10 enterprise customers and 40+ resellers. Sold AgileGraph to the US Army.
  • Raised $14M in private investment.
  • At Lumen, grew the CenturyLink app from 800K to 1.4M+ users and raised its rating from 2.4 to 4.4.

10 of 10

The Next 12 Months

  • 5,000 families and 1,000 Looop Plus subscribers.

Peace of mind for you, independence for them.
