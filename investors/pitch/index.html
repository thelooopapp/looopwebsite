I’m GenX.

The sandwich generation.
  • I’m responsible for aging parents and college students.
  • My family is in 5 cities in 3 states.
  • I use multiple apps for location tracking, weather, crime, spending, health and more.
  • I don’t need all that.

I just need to know when to check in on them. And when to give them their independence.

I’m not alone. There are 63 million family caregivers like me.

…so I built what we all need.

Looop puts it all

in one place

Each day, Looop gives one answer about each Loved One: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

  • Learns each Loved One’s own normal, and speaks up only when something looks off
  • Holistic family care: health, fitness, spending, activity, weather, crime, and the shape of each day
  • Alerts instead of a map to watch
  • One tap to call, text, or 911
  • No ads, and your family’s data is never for sale

  • Sleep, Deep, REM, and naps
  • Heart rate, daily high and low
  • Breathing rate
  • Steps, walking and workouts
  • Time in loud places

Shared only if they opt-in. Data never sold, never used for ads.
