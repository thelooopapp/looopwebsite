Is Everyone Okay Today?

My family of five lives in five different cities. Looop answers that question at a glance, any time of day, from the phones and watches they already have.

Jeff Carpenter, founder · Austin, Texas · jeff@thelooopapp.com

2 of 10

Distributed Families Are Hard to Keep Up With

  • Keeping up means checking the weather in five cities, a map in one app, and crime alerts in another.
  • 64% of family caregivers report high emotional stress (AARP/NAC 2025).
  • Most family apps are built around a map. These families want to know whether everyone is okay today.

3 of 10

The Leaders Are Moving Here

  • Life360: 102.4 million monthly users and 3.2 million paying family groups. It has named aging parents as a next line of business.
  • Luffu: Launched by Fitbit's founders in February 2026, self-funded, with a $299.99 band shipping in early 2027.
  • 78% of Americans 65 and older own a smartphone, and 24% have a smartwatch (Pew Research Center).

4 of 10

The Other Options Each Cover One Part

  • Life360: A live family map, driving safety, crash detection, and weather and crime alerts.
  • Find My: Location, with arrive and leave notifications.
  • Medical alert services: Response once there's an emergency.
  • Luffu: A family health app, with a band to buy and wear coming next.

Looop gives families one answer about each Loved One that they can check any time, and one place to look for weather, crime, arrivals, and unusual spending.

5 of 10

63 Million Caregivers and Growing

  • 63 million US family caregivers, up 45% in a decade (AARP/NAC 2025).
  • At $49.99 a year per caregiver who wants alerts, about $3.2 billion.
  • By 2030, every baby boomer will be 65 or older. Older adults are projected to outnumber children by 2034 (US Census Bureau).

6 of 10

One Answer About Each Loved One

Looop sends alerts about health, fitness, spending, activity, weather, crime, and the shape of each day. All of it comes together in one answer about each Loved One, kept current through the day: A Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

  • Severe weather: Storms, floods, and extreme heat or cold, in more than 50 countries.
  • Nearby crime: At the distance the family chooses, in all 50 US states.
  • Safe arrivals: At saved places, hospitals, police stations, and airports.
  • Inactivity: A phone untouched for hours, or three days at home.
  • Unusual spending: A large charge on a linked account, an early sign of a scam.
  • Health: Unusual heart rate, breathing rate, or sleep, through Apple Health.
  • Fitness: Steps and workouts that fall off from someone's usual days.

7 of 10

One Easy Answer, Any Time of Day

  • Looop learns each Loved One's own normal for activity, health, and phone use, and combines it with weather, crime, and spending alerts.
  • Reliable background updates made Life360 the leader. Looop is built the same way: It keeps working in the background, after a force-close, after a restart, and right after a dead battery is charged.
  • 81% of Americans are concerned about how companies use their data (Pew, 2023). Looop makes money from subscriptions alone, so family data is never sold or used for ads.

8 of 10

Live and Ready to Grow

  • Live on iPhone since May 2026, with early families as our usability and UI testers.
  • 417 product updates shipped since March 30, 2026.
  • In a June 2026 usability test run with a small Meta ad campaign, 78% finished setup. Sign-up now requires an accepted invite.
  • Pre-marketing and pre-revenue, with Looop Plus and the free trial live.

9 of 10

Anyone Can View for Free

  • Anyone in the family can see everyone's status for free. Each person who wants push alerts subscribes to Looop Plus: $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year.
  • The 14-day free trial starts when a second person joins, and every Loved One joins by invitation.
  • Life360's paid plans run $9.99 to $24.99 a month per family. Luffu Link is $299.99 plus $19.99 a month. Looop needs nothing new to buy or wear.
