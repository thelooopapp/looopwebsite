Choosing between Looop and Luffu?

Both are new, and both are built for the person who looks out for everyone: a parent a few states away, kids at college, a partner, a sibling.

Luffu, from Fitbit's founders, is a family health app that reads the wearables you already own, with a new, optional LTE band called Luffu Link.

Looop is a safety notifications app that runs on the iPhone and watch your family already has, and adds weather, crime, and spending to the same daily answer. It never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers.

Below, we compare the two with sources, including where Luffu may be the better fit.

What Luffu does well

Luffu comes from a team that knows wearables, and it does a lot for the family member who manages everyone's health.

Built by Fitbit's founders

James Park and Eric Friedman started Fitbit in 2007 and announced Luffu in February 2026. They've built wearables before, and it shows in the band's design.

Works with wearables you already own

Luffu's app reads Apple Health, Fitbit, and other wearables, so a family can start without buying anything new.

Medications, meals, and doctor visits

Families can log medications, meals, symptoms, medical records, and care plans by voice, text, or photo, and see them next to health readings.

A band that works without a phone

Luffu Link has LTE and GPS built in, with no carrier plan needed. A parent can press a button to send a help message, or double-tap to say they're okay.

The whole household

Luffu covers kids, partners, aging parents, and pets, for the person who keeps up with everyone's health.

Questions in plain language

You can ask about your family's health information and get answers, insights, or charts you can save and share.

Where Looop and Luffu part ways

Six differences, with sources.

  • Safety alerts from the phone she already carries

    Looop's safety alerts run on the iPhone your Loved One already carries: arrivals at a hospital, police station, or airport, a drive or a walk that looks unusual, a phone untouched for hours, and low battery. There's no band to order, set up, or charge.

    For health readings, the two are alike: Luffu's app also reads Apple Health, Fitbit, and other wearables. For safety, Luffu lists help messages, check-ins, and location sharing as features of Luffu Link, a $299.99 band ($249.99 on preorder) that needs a Luffu Family Plan and is expected to ship in early 2027.

  • On the App Store today

    Looop launched on the App Store in May 2026, and families can download it today.

    Luffu announced its app in February 2026 as a beta. Its Luffu Link page says Family Plans will be available "later this year."

  • A heads-up about an unusually large charge

    If you link a bank account, Looop can let you know about unusually large spending, with a threshold you set. A sudden large payment can be an early sign of a scam, so you can call Mom that same day. Bank linking is optional, runs through Plaid, and is never used for ads or sold.

    We didn't find money or spending features listed on Luffu's site.

  • Storms and crime near Mom, in the same answer

    Looop tells you when severe weather heads toward a Loved One, in more than 50 countries, and when crime is reported nearby, in all 50 US states, at the distance your family picks: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.

    Luffu focuses on health, and we didn't find weather or crime alerts listed on luffu.com or the Luffu Link page.

    In Looop, weather and crime are part of a holistic approach to family care: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day. Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

  • Alerts that run on their own

    Looop doesn't ask anyone to log anything. Once a Loved One joins, it learns their routine and speaks up when a day looks different.

    Luffu also flags changes on its own, and it goes further as a family health record: medications, meals, symptoms, and doctor visits that your family logs by voice, text, or photo. Its day in the life page shows a Morning Brief, a Concierge that plans dinner around everyone's diet, and a Family Retrospective at night. Some families will love that.

  • No advertising partners

    Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads and no sponsored offers in your notifications.

    Luffu's privacy notice (last updated August 23, 2026) says it does not "sell" consumer health data, as defined under state health privacy laws. The same notice says, "We may share your non-health personal information with third-party advertising partners."

We want you to pick the app that fits your family.

A parent who doesn't carry a smartphone

Looop runs on the Loved One's iPhone. Luffu Link stays connected over LTE with no phone nearby, in the US.

Android families

Looop is iPhone only today. Luffu lists its band as working with iPhone and Android.

A family health record

If you're the one keeping up with prescriptions, meals, symptoms, and doctor visits, Luffu is built to log them all in one place.

One button for help

Luffu Link sends a voice message and location to trusted contacts at the press of a button. Looop puts 911 and emergency contacts one tap away on the phone.

Kids, partners, and pets in one place

If you want the whole household's health in one app, pets included, Luffu covers that. Looop is only for people.

Answers about your family's health

If you'd like to ask questions about your family's health records in plain language, Luffu offers that. Looop doesn't.

Questions families ask

Is Looop or Luffu better for my family?

Looop is built for families who care from a distance: parents living separately or a few states away, a relative living alone, kids at college, or siblings and friends who look out for each other. It tells you when to check in. Choose Luffu if you want a family health record you keep by voice, text, or photo, kids and pets in the same app, and a $299.99 band with a help button for a parent who doesn't carry a phone. Choose Looop if you want safety alerts from the iPhone she already carries, weather, crime, and spending in the same daily answer, an app on the App Store today, and your family's data never for sale.

Do I need to buy anything to use Looop?

No. Looop runs on iPhone. If a Loved One adds Health & Fitness sharing, Looop reads their Apple Watch, Oura Ring, or Whoop through Apple Health. Looop is free for the first two weeks.

Does Luffu work without its band?

Yes. Luffu's app reads Apple Health, Fitbit, and other wearables, and your family can add logs by voice, text, or photo. Luffu Link adds its own health sensors, LTE so it works with no phone nearby in the US, a button for help messages to trusted contacts, a double-tap check-in, location sharing, and voice logging. The band is $299.99 ($249.99 on preorder), needs a Luffu Family Plan, and is expected to ship in early 2027.

Can Looop or Luffu call 911 for us?

Neither one calls 911 for you. Looop lets you call 911 in one tap and save up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One. Luffu Link sends help messages to the trusted contacts you set up, and Luffu says it doesn't call 911. Looop helps you know when to check in. It doesn't detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

Does Looop send weather and crime alerts?

Yes. Severe weather alerts work in more than 50 countries, and crime alerts work in all 50 US states, at the distance you choose: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.

Does Looop work on Android?

Not today. Looop is available for iPhone on the App Store. If your family uses Android phones, Luffu may be a better fit.

Does Looop sell my data?

No. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads in the app and no sponsored offers in your notifications. You can delete your account and data whenever you choose.

Will my mom know she's in our Looop?

Yes. Every Loved One joins by invitation. Health & Fitness sharing, like heart rate, sleep, steps, and workouts, is available and optional.
