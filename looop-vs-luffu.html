Looking at Looop and Luffu?

Looop is a safety notifications app for families who care from a distance. Health is one part of it. Looop also tells you about severe weather, nearby crime, an unusually large charge, a trip to the ER, and a day that doesn't look like their usual, all in one daily answer.

Luffu is a family health app. It keeps medications, records, and vital signs in one shared place, alerts you to changes in health, and has a $299.99 band, Luffu Link, coming in early 2027.

Below, how the two compare, including where Luffu may be the better fit.

What Luffu does well

Luffu comes from the founders of Fitbit, and it's built around health devices, including the one it sells.

A band that works without a phone nearby

Luffu Link has its own LTE and GPS, a help button for trusted contacts, a double-tap check-in, and up to five days of battery, in the US, expected early 2027.

A shared health record

Medications, meals, medical records, vital signs, and doctor visits, added by voice, text, photo, or document, and shared with the parents, spouses, and caregivers you choose, with control over who can see or update what.

Alerts that run on their own

Luffu watches for missed medications and changes in health and activity, and alerts you before they become concerns.

Answers in plain language

Ask about the family's health information and get answers, insights, and charts you can adjust, save, and share.

"Isn't health enough?"

Where Looop and Luffu part ways

Seven ways they differ.

  • Health, plus everything else

    Looop reads the same health and fitness data from the watch they already wear. Then it adds the rest of their day: severe weather, nearby crime, an unusually large charge, a trip to the ER, a drive or a walk that looks unusual. Each day, all of it comes together in one answer about each Loved One: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

    Luffu covers health. It keeps medications, meals, medical records, and vital signs, and alerts you to changes in health and activity. For a family managing a diagnosis, that depth is the point. It's also one piece of what Looop covers.

  • Looop is automatic

    A Loved One accepts an invitation once. After that, Looop is automatic: quiet when a day looks like their own normal, and a heads-up when it doesn't. There's no record to keep.

    Luffu is built around a record your family keeps: medications, meals, medical records, and vital signs, added by voice, text, photo, or document, with answers to questions about what's there. For a family managing a diagnosis, that record is the point. For a family that wants to know everyone's okay, it's one more thing to do.

  • Severe weather and nearby crime

    Looop tells you when severe weather is heading toward a Loved One, in more than 50 countries, and when crime is reported nearby, in all 50 US states, at the distance your family picks: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles. Instead of checking the weather in five cities, families get one heads-up when it matters.

    Luffu doesn't offer weather or crime alerts. The app covers health, and the $299.99 Luffu Link band adds location sharing and a help button, expected early 2027.

  • A heads-up about an unusually large charge

    When a Loved One links a bank account, Looop lets you know about an unusually large charge, with a threshold you set. A sudden large payment can be an early sign of a scam, so you can call that same day. Looop never sells that information.

    Luffu doesn't offer spending alerts. Its focus is health: medications, meals, medical records, and vital signs.

  • Help in an emergency

    Looop puts 911 and up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One one tap away, on the phone they already carry. Looop doesn't detect emergencies, so if you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

    Luffu Link doesn't call 911 either. Its button texts the trusted contacts you set up, and a double tap lets family know you're okay. The band works in the US only.

  • Advertisers and your data

    Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads and no sponsored offers in your notifications. Your family's data isn't for sale.

    Luffu doesn't sell health data. Its privacy notice (August 23, 2026) says it "may share your non-health personal information with third-party advertising partners."

  • You can download Looop today

    Looop launched on the App Store in May 2026. Download it, sign up with your phone number, and invite your family from your contacts.

    Luffu announced its app in February 2026 as a beta with a waitlist. It isn't on the App Store yet. Full release is expected later this year, and the band is expected to ship in early 2027.

Questions families ask

Is Looop or Luffu better for our family?

Looop is built for families who care from a distance: parents a few states away, a relative living alone, kids at college, or siblings and friends who look out for each other. It tells you when to check in, about the whole of a day. Luffu is built for the caregiver who keeps up with the family's medications, records, and doctor visits in one place, with alerts when health or activity changes. If your family needs a health record, Luffu is built for that. If you want one automatic daily answer, that's Looop.

Can one Looop cover a parent in another state and a kid at college?

Yes. Each Loved One gets their own daily answer, based on their own routine. A storm heading toward your parents' town and a crime report near a campus show up in the same app, each at the distance you choose.

Does Looop help with medications?

No. Looop keeps no medication list, care plan, or records. If that's the job, Luffu is built for it.

Does anyone in our family have to do anything each day?

No. Each Loved One accepts an invitation once. After that, Looop is automatic. Say your dad usually drives to golf on Tuesdays and this week his phone hasn't moved by noon. That's the kind of day Looop would flag.

Do our parents need an Apple Watch for Looop?

No. Looop works from the iPhone they already carry. Health & Fitness sharing is available if they wear an Apple Watch, Oura Ring, or Whoop, and it's their choice.

Does Luffu work without its band?

Yes. Luffu's app reads Apple Health and Fitbit, flags health and activity changes on its own, and keeps the records your family adds. Luffu Link adds its own health sensors, LTE so it works with no phone nearby in the US, a button for help messages to trusted contacts, a double-tap check-in, location sharing, and voice entry. The band is $299.99 ($249.99 on preorder), needs a Luffu Family Plan, and is expected to ship in early 2027.

Can Looop or Luffu call 911 for us?

Neither one calls 911 for you. Looop puts 911 and up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One one tap away. Luffu Link's button texts the contacts you set up. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

Does Looop send weather and crime alerts?

Yes. Severe weather alerts work in more than 50 countries, and crime alerts work in all 50 US states, at the distance you choose: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.

Does Looop work on Android?

Not yet. Looop is on the App Store for iPhone.

Does Looop sell our data?

No. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads in the app and no sponsored offers in your notifications. You can delete your account and data whenever you choose.
