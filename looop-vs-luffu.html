Choosing between Looop and Luffu?

What Luffu does well

Luffu comes from a team that knows wearables, and it does a lot for the family member who manages everyone's health.

Built by Fitbit's founders

James Park and Eric Friedman started Fitbit in 2007. They announced Luffu in February 2026, and they know how to make a band people wear every day.

Medications, meals, and doctor visits

Families can log medications, meals, symptoms, and care plans by voice, text, or photo, and see them next to health readings.

Works without a phone nearby

Luffu Link has LTE and GPS built in, with no carrier plan needed, so a parent can send a help or check-in message from the wrist.

The whole household

Luffu covers kids, partners, aging parents, and pets, for the person who keeps track of everyone's health.

Questions in plain language

You can ask the app about your family's health information and get answers, insights, or charts you can save and share.

iPhone and Android

Luffu lists the Link band as working with iPhone and Android phones.

"Do I really need to buy Mom a new band?"

Where Looop and Luffu part ways

Seven differences, with sources.

  • Nothing new to buy, nothing new to charge

    Looop runs on the iPhone your Loved One already carries. If they add Health & Fitness sharing, it reads their Apple Watch, Oura Ring, or Whoop through Apple Health. There's no band to order, set up, or charge.

    Luffu's app also reads Apple Health and Fitbit. Its new band, Luffu Link, is $299.99, or $249.99 on preorder. The band requires a Luffu Family Plan, sold separately, at $19.99 a month for up to four people, and its battery lasts up to five days with GPS off.

  • On the App Store today

    Looop launched on the App Store in May 2026, and families can download it today.

    Luffu announced its app in February 2026 as a limited beta with a waitlist. Its order page says the app will be available "later this year," and Luffu Link is expected to ship in early 2027.

  • A heads-up about an unusually large charge

    If you link a bank account, Looop can let you know about unusually large spending, with a threshold you set. A sudden large payment can be an early sign of a scam, so you can call Mom that same day. Bank linking is optional, runs through Plaid, and is never used for ads or sold.

    We didn't find money or spending features listed on Luffu's site.

  • Storms and crime near Mom, in the same answer

    Looop tells you when severe weather heads toward a Loved One, in more than 50 countries, and when crime is reported nearby, in all 50 US states, at the distance your family picks: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.

    Luffu's site describes health tracking, help messages, check-ins, and location sharing. We didn't find weather or crime alerts listed on luffu.com or the Luffu Link page.

    In Looop, weather and crime are part of a holistic approach to family care: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day. Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

  • Alerts that travel with them

    Mom's winter in Florida, Dad's wine tour in Italy, a semester in Spain: Looop's weather alerts work in more than 50 countries.

    Luffu says on its order page that, for now, Luffu Link "only works within the United States."

  • No advertising partners

    Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads and no sponsored offers in your notifications.

    Luffu's privacy notice (last updated August 23, 2026) says it does not "sell" consumer health data, as defined under state health privacy laws. The same notice says, "We may share your non-health personal information with third-party advertising partners."

  • Looop sticks to one job

    Looop is built to tell you when to check in, and it uses only what it needs to tell whether something unusual is going on.

    Luffu is aiming wider. Its day in the life page shows a Morning Brief, medication and meal logs, a Concierge that plans dinner around everyone's diet, a dog's walks, and a Family Retrospective at night. A home device, Luffu Frame, is coming. Some families will love that.

Questions families ask

Is Looop or Luffu better for my family?

Looop is built for families who care from a distance: parents living separately or a few states away, a relative living alone, kids at college, or siblings and friends who look out for each other. It tells you when to check in, using the phone and watch your family already has. Choose Luffu if you want a new $299.99 band for Mom to wear, another device for her to charge, a Family Plan to keep it connected, and a band that ships in early 2027 and works only in the US. Choose Looop if you want nothing new to buy, the iPhone and watch she already has, alerts on the App Store today, and your family's data never for sale.

Do I need to buy anything to use Looop?

No. Looop runs on iPhone. If a Loved One adds Health & Fitness sharing, Looop reads their Apple Watch, Oura Ring, or Whoop through Apple Health. Looop is free for the first two weeks.

Does Luffu need its band?

Luffu's app also reads Apple Health and Fitbit. Luffu Link and the Family Plan it requires are sold separately. The band is $299.99 ($249.99 on preorder) and is expected to ship in early 2027.

Can Looop or Luffu call 911 for us?

Neither one calls 911 for you. Looop lets you call 911 in one tap and save up to three emergency contacts for each Loved One. Luffu Link sends help messages to the trusted contacts you set up, and Luffu says it doesn't call 911. Looop helps you know when to check in. It doesn't detect emergencies. If you think someone is in danger, call 911 first.

Does Looop send weather and crime alerts?

Yes. Severe weather alerts work in more than 50 countries, and crime alerts work in all 50 US states, at the distance you choose: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.

Does Looop work on Android?

Not today. Looop is available for iPhone on the App Store. If your family uses Android phones, Luffu may be a better fit for now.

Does Looop sell my data?

No. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads in the app and no sponsored offers in your notifications. You can delete your account and data whenever you choose.

Will my mom know she's in our Looop?

Yes. Every Loved One joins by invitation. Health & Fitness sharing, like heart rate, sleep, steps, and workouts, is available and optional.
