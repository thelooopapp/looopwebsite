They're on their own now. You'll still know if something seems off.

Give your college kid space without losing sleep.

When your kids are away at college, you want two things at once: for them to have real independence, and for you to know they're okay. Looop gives you both. It sends a heads-up about crime reported near campus or a storm heading their way, and stays quiet the rest of the time.

"Did she get home okay?"

Calm alerts, so you know when to reach out

  • Crime alerts near campus. A heads-up when something is reported nearby, in supported cities.
  • Severe weather. Storms, flood warnings and extreme heat or cold near their school, during finals week or any week.
  • Unusual Day. A gentle nudge when their day looks very different from their own normal.
  • Low battery. Know when their phone is about to run out of charge, so a missed text doesn't send you spiraling.
  • Airport arrivals. Know when they land for winter break.

One calm text instead of ten anxious ones.

One app for everyone you love.

Plenty of parents are worried about college kids and their own parents at the same time. Add everyone to one Looop. You'll see who is having a typical day at a glance, and get one heads-up when someone needs a call.

"I moved out. Please trust me."

A word for your student

Your parents stop texting every hour. You keep living your life.

Looop isn't a curfew. It doesn't send your parents a play-by-play of your day. It only speaks up when something seems off, like a storm near campus or a day that looks really unusual for you.

  • It's an invite, not a requirement. You decide whether to join.
  • Health details are optional. Share them only if you want to.
  • You can leave a group anytime.
  • It works both ways. Want a heads-up if a storm hits back home? You get that too.

Make it a conversation

Before move-in day, talk about what Looop shares and why. Agree on what a check-in looks like. Maybe a quick "you okay?" text after an alert, and the usual Sunday call for everything else.

Give them space. Keep your peace of mind.
