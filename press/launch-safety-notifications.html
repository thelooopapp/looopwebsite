Looop Officially Launches a Safety Notifications App for Families Who Care From a Distance

Press release·October 19, 2026

The new safety notifications app tells families when to check in on Loved Ones near and far, and pledges to never sell data or work with advertisers

AUSTIN, Texas, October 19, 2026: LooopApp LLC today officially launched Looop, a safety notifications app for families, friends, and Loved Ones who care for each other from a distance. After a soft launch on the App Store in May, Looop now sends severe weather alerts in more than 50 countries and nearby crime alerts in all 50 US states. Looop sends families a heads-up when something might need their attention, so they know when a call is worth making.

Many adults now care for people in several places at once: kids at college in one state, a parent living alone in another, siblings and friends spread across the country. Looop is built for these families, whose relationships run on trust and independence.

"We built Looop for families who live far apart and still want to know how everyone's doing. It tells you when a call is worth making, and everyone else gets on with their day. Anyone with an iPhone can download it and invite the people they care about," said the team behind Looop.

A new category: safety notifications

Looop learns each Loved One's usual routine and sends a notification when something looks unusual. Key features include:

  • Severe weather alerts: A heads-up when severe weather is heading toward a Loved One, in more than 50 countries, including the US.
  • Nearby crime alerts: A notification when crime is reported near a Loved One, in all 50 US states, with an alert distance each family chooses.
  • Unusual activity nudges: An "Unusual Day" alert when driving, walking, or phone use looks different from that person's own normal routine.
  • Daily reassurance at a glance: One simple screen that shows who is having a typical day and who might need a look, in plain language.
  • Health & Fitness sharing, available and optional: Heart rate, breathing rate, and sleep, plus steps, walks, and workouts.
  • One-tap check-ins: Call, text, or FaceTime a Loved One, or send a quick "Love you!", straight from their card.

A family map is available as a supporting feature, for the moment after a nudge when a family wants more context.

Peace of mind for you, independence for them

Every Loved One joins Looop by invitation, and their day goes on as usual. Looop runs in the background and reaches out to the family only when something looks unusual, which means fewer "just checking" calls for everyone.

A privacy pledge

Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads and no sponsored offers in its notifications. Looop uses only what it needs to tell whether something unusual is going on.

Availability

Looop is available now for iPhone on the App Store at https://apps.apple.com/us/app/looop-keep-loved-ones-safe/id6760598111. Learn more at https://www.thelooopapp.com.

About Looop

Looop is a safety notifications app that helps families know their Loved Ones are safe, across any distance. It sends safety alerts instead of tracking: a heads-up about nearby crime, severe weather, and unusual activity, so families know when to check in and Loved Ones keep their independence. Looop has no ads, ever, and your family's data is never for sale. Learn more at https://www.thelooopapp.com.
