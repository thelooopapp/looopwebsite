Austin Startup Already Ships Aging-Parent Alerts While a Public Company Is Still Promising Them

Draft for review·Placeholders in brackets are not final

Life360 has spent a year promising new aging-parent features. Looop launched in May 2026 and keeps shipping more.

AUSTIN, Texas, Oct. 27, 2026: LooopApp LLC, an Austin startup, today announced that Looop now provides safety alerts about severe weather in more than 50 countries and nearby crime alerts in all 50 US states to keep your loved ones safe, wherever they go. Looop is a safety notifications app for families who care from a distance, built by an independent startup that answers to families, not advertisers. It sends a heads-up when a loved one's day looks different from their own routine, so families know when a call is worth making.

In October 2025, Life360 CEO Lauren Antonoff discussed on Fox Business how the company is exploring features for aging parents. On its Q4 2025 earnings call in March 2026, the company said it was working on devices for aging parents but did not expect to launch them that year. On its Q2 2026 call in August, it named aging parents as a next line of business. And at a Goldman Sachs conference in September, it said it is working on services for aging parents, including wellness features, scam protection and emergency response.

Looop's aging-parent alerts are already here. Looop launched on the App Store in May 2026 and has shipped new features and updates steadily since.

Looop's alerts are built around one question: "Is Mom okay today?"

Alerts live in Looop today

  • Severe weather and nearby crime (new): severe weather alerts in more than 50 countries, and nearby crime alerts in all 50 US states.
  • Unusual Day: a nudge when something is off compared to a loved one's own normal routine.
  • Phone untouched for hours: a heads-up when their phone hasn't been picked up.
  • Unusual spending: an optional alert for unusually large spending on a linked bank account, which can be an early sign of a scam. Bank linking is optional and runs through Plaid.
  • Arrivals that matter: know when a loved one arrives at a hospital or ER, a police station or an airport.
  • Optional health alerts: unusually high or low heart rate or an unusual breathing rate, shared only if the loved one chooses, through Apple Health and Apple Watch.

Every loved one joins Looop by invitation and decides whether to share health details or link a bank account.

"We built Looop because our own families are spread across the country and around the world. We're Gen X, looking after kids in college and aging parents at the same time. We don't want to track them. We want them to keep their independence, and we want to know when it's time to call, so we're ready to help," said the team behind Looop.

Private by design

Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. Its notifications carry no ads or sponsored offers.

Availability

Looop is available now for iPhone on the App Store at https://apps.apple.com/us/app/looop-keep-loved-ones-safe/id6760598111. Learn more at https://www.thelooopapp.com.

About Looop

Looop is a safety notifications app that helps families know their loved ones are safe, across any distance. It sends safety alerts instead of tracking: a heads-up about nearby crime, severe weather and unusual activity, so families know when to check in and loved ones keep their independence. Looop has no ads, ever, and your family's data is never for sale. Learn more at https://www.thelooopapp.com.
