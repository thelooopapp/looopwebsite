Looop Is a New Kind of Family Safety App, Not Another Map

Draft for review·Placeholders in brackets are not final

An independent Austin startup gives families one daily answer about the people they love

AUSTIN, Texas, [DATE]: Looop, a safety notifications app from LooopApp LLC, is a new kind of family safety app that tells families when it might be a good time to check in on a loved one. Looop launched on the App Store in May 2026 for iPhone.

Most family apps are built around a map: they answer "Where is she?"
Looop answers "Is Mom okay today?"

Looop takes a holistic approach to family care: weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day. No more checking the weather in five cities, a map in one app, and bank alerts in another. It's all in one place.

  • Severe weather alerts in more than 50 countries, when a storm, flood warning, or extreme heat or cold could affect a loved one.
  • Nearby crime alerts in all 50 US states, at the distance each family chooses: 1, 3, 5, or 10 miles.
  • Optional spending alerts: a heads-up about an unusually large charge on a linked account, which can be an early sign of a scam. Bank linking runs through Plaid and is never used for ads or sold.
  • Health & Fitness sharing through Apple Health, with devices like Apple Watch, Oura, and Whoop, is available if a family needs it.
  • A gentle nudge when driving, walking, or phone activity looks unusual.

Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call. When all is well, Looop stays quiet.

"Families are spread across the country, and none of us wanted to watch a dot on a map to know if Mom was okay. We want a nudge when it's worth making a call, and to leave everyone alone the rest of the time," said the team behind Looop.

Built for families who care from a distance

Looop is built for adult children caring for a parent who lives far away, and for families, siblings, and friends spread across states and borders. Every loved one joins by invitation and keeps their routine and their independence. Looop works alongside Find My and the other tools already on a parent's iPhone.

Looop isn't an emergency service, and it doesn't call 911 or send help. It helps families know when to check in.

Private by design

Looop has no ads, ever, and doesn't work with advertisers. Your family's data is never for sale. Looop's App Store privacy label has no "Data Used to Track You" section and doesn't list the app as containing advertising.

About Looop

Looop is a safety notifications app that helps families know their loved ones are safe, across any distance. It brings holistic family care to the people you love: safety alerts instead of tracking, where weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day come together in one answer, so families know when to check in and loved ones keep their independence. Looop has no ads, ever, and your family's data is never for sale. Learn more at https://www.thelooopapp.com.
