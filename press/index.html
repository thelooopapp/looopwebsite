About Looop

Looop is a safety notifications app that helps families know their loved ones are safe, across any distance. Looop sends a heads-up about nearby crime, severe weather and unusual activity, so families know when to check in and loved ones keep their independence. Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. Learn more at thelooopapp.com.
