Austin Solo Founder Ships the Aging-Parent Alerts a Public Company Is Still Promising

Draft for review·Placeholders in brackets are not final

Looop, a safety notifications app built by one founder, already tells families when a parent's day looks unusual, while Life360 tells investors aging-parent features are on its roadmap

AUSTIN, Texas - [DATE] - Looop, LLC today highlighted the aging-parent alerts already live in Looop, a safety notifications app for families who care from a distance. Looop was built by a single founder in Austin [CONFIRM: using AI tools]. It sends a calm heads-up when a Loved One's day looks different from their own routine, so families know when a call is worth making.

The news comes as Life360 turns its attention to aging parents. In October 2025, Life360 CEO Lauren Antonoff discussed on Fox Business how the company is exploring features for aging parents. On its Q4 2025 earnings call in March 2026, the company said it was working on devices for aging parents but did not expect to launch them that year. On its Q2 2026 call in August, it named aging parents as a next line of business. And at a Goldman Sachs conference in September, it said it is working on services for aging parents, including wellness checks and scam protection.

Looop families already get alerts built around that same worry: "Is Mom okay today?"

Alerts live in Looop today

  • Unusual Day (live since [LAUNCH DATE: Unusual Day alert]): a gentle nudge when something is off compared to a Loved One's own normal routine.
  • Phone untouched for hours (live since [LAUNCH DATE: phone untouched alert]): a heads-up when their phone hasn't been picked up in a long while.
  • Unusual spending (live since [LAUNCH DATE: unusual spending alert]): an optional alert for unusually large spending on a linked bank account, which can be an early sign of a scam. Bank linking is optional and runs through Plaid.
  • Arrivals that matter (live since [LAUNCH DATE: hospital arrival alert]): know when a Loved One arrives at a hospital or ER, a police station or an airport.
  • Optional health alerts (live since [LAUNCH DATE: heart rate and breathing rate alerts]): unusually high or low heart rate or an unusual breathing rate, shared only if the Loved One chooses, through Apple Health and Apple Watch.
  • Severe weather and nearby crime (live since [LAUNCH DATE: severe weather alerts] and [LAUNCH DATE: nearby crime alerts]): severe weather alerts in the US and worldwide, and crime alerts in more than 200 US cities and counties.

Every Loved One joins Looop by invitation. They keep their routine and their independence. Their family gets fewer reasons to worry, and fewer "just checking" calls to make.

"[DRAFT QUOTE - FOUNDER TO APPROVE] My question was never where my mom was. It was whether her day looked like her day. Big companies are right that aging parents matter. Families shouldn't have to wait for a roadmap. Looop does one job: it tells you when to check in," said [FOUNDER NAME], founder of Looop.

No ads, no data sales

Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. There are no ads and no sponsored offers in its notifications.
