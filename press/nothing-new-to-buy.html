Looop brings aging-parent alerts to the phone and watch a parent already owns, with nothing new to buy

Press release·Draft, not yet released

New wearables for aging parents are on preorder for 2027. Looop is on the App Store today and works with iPhone, Apple Watch, Oura, and Whoop.

AUSTIN, Texas, [RELEASE DATE]: LooopApp LLC, an Austin startup, said today that families can get alerts about a parent's health, fitness, unusual spending, activity, severe weather, and nearby crime without buying a new device. Looop runs on the iPhone a parent already carries. If the parent chooses to share, it reads the Apple Watch, Oura Ring, or Whoop they already wear, through Apple Health. There's nothing new for the parent to wear or charge.

Families caring for a parent from a distance are being offered more hardware this year. In August, Luffu, a company started by Fitbit's co-founders, opened preorders for Luffu Link, a screenless LTE band for the person being cared for. Luffu lists the band at $299.99, or $249.99 on preorder, with a required Family Plan of $19.99 a month for up to four people, and expects it to ship in early 2027.

Looop launched on the App Store in May 2026, and families can download it today.

Most family apps are built around a map: they answer "Where is she?" Looop answers the question families actually ask: "Is Mom okay today?"

Looop brings health, fitness, spending, activity, weather, crime, and the shape of each day into one place, so families aren't piecing it together from a health app, a banking app, and the weather in five cities.

  • Her health: heart rate, breathing, and sleep, through Apple Health and devices like Apple Watch, Oura, and Whoop.
  • How she's moving: steps, walks, and workouts, from the same devices.
  • Unusual spending: a heads-up about unusually large spending on a linked account, which can be an early sign of a scam.
  • What's changed: walking, driving, time spent in one place, phone use that's higher or lower than usual, and a phone left untouched for hours.
  • Where she is: a heads-up when she arrives at a hospital or ER, a police station, or an airport, and a map for when you need it.
  • What's around her: severe weather in more than 50 countries and nearby crime in all 50 US states.

Each day, all of it comes together in one answer: a Typical Day, or an Unusual Day that might be worth a call.

Every loved one joins Looop by invitation and decides whether to add Health & Fitness sharing or link a bank account.

"Most of our parents already carry an iPhone, and a lot of them already wear a watch or a ring. We didn't want to give Mom one more thing to wear and remember to charge. Looop works with what she already has, and it tells us when it's worth a call," said the team behind Looop.

How the two compare

Luffu offers things Looop doesn't, including a band that stays connected over LTE with no phone nearby, logs for medications, meals, and doctor visits, and support for Android phones. Looop adds unusual spending, severe weather, and nearby crime to the same daily answer, works in more than 50 countries, and uses devices a family already owns. A side-by-side comparison, with sources, is at thelooopapp.com/looop-vs-luffu.html.

Private by design

Looop never sells your data and doesn't work with advertisers. Its notifications carry no ads or sponsored offers.

Availability

Looop is available now for iPhone on the App Store at https://apps.apple.com/us/app/looop-keep-loved-ones-safe/id6760598111. Learn more at https://www.thelooopapp.com.

About Looop

Looop is a safety notifications app that helps families know their loved ones are safe, across any distance. It brings holistic family care to the people you love: safety alerts instead of tracking, where weather, crime, spending, health, fitness, activity, and the shape of each day come together in one answer, so families know when to check in and loved ones keep their independence. Looop has no ads, ever, and your family's data is never for sale. Learn more at https://www.thelooopapp.com.
